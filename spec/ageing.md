# Ageing, peak age and new talent

Written by the main session at promotion (docs/wide-pass.md, the wide pass of 2026-10-13): rows drafted from reviewed findings F001-F022 by read-only agents, statuses re-derived by the main session (`corroborated` only with two findings and both a behavioural and a static method in the Basis and Findings cells). The summary describes only `supported` and `corroborated` claims.

## Summary

Players age at the ageing gate, entered when the stage byte becomes 2, 3, 5 or 7 (four times per 4-year cycle, C-AGE-01). `1771:80DA` visits countries 1..128; for each it ages the 20 squad players and, for a managed country, the manager's 80 pool players (C-AGE-02). A continuing player's update writes only +1 (age + 1), +4 (recomputed) and +0x11 (cleared), uses no random number and never changes peak age +2, ceiling +3, profile +5 or class +7 (C-AGE-03). The ability rule: with a = age after the increment, p = peak, r = ceiling, +4 = r for p-1 <= a <= p+3, falling by r/16 per year after p+3 and rising by r/16 per two years before p-1 (C-AGE-04, corroborated). Computer countries retire a player when (age > 30, or 33 for a goalkeeper, and caps = 0) or age > peak + 5, and re-create him in place with ten random draws (C-AGE-08, C-AGE-09); managed countries replace one retiree at a time between fixtures (C-AGE-12). Replacements get ceiling rating + 0x7F, so results reach new talent only through the ranks (C-AGE-10, C-AGE-11).
