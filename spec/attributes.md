# Player attributes and skills

Written by the main session at promotion (docs/wide-pass.md, the wide pass of 2026-10-13): rows drafted from reviewed findings F001-F022 by read-only agents, statuses re-derived by the main session (`corroborated` only with two findings and both a behavioural and a static method in the Basis and Findings cells). The summary describes only `supported` and `corroborated` claims.

## Summary

The player record is 20 bytes; no skill is stored (C-ATTR-01, C-ATTR-02). The 12 skills are computed from three bytes, +4 (ability, the scale), +5 (profile 1..16) and +7 (position class): `skill_i = DS:6DCD[12*(+5) + c] * ((+4)+1) div 256`, `c = DS:6D25[12*(+7) + i]` on the player screen (bars 0..25), and with divisor 32 inside the engines through `1771:3A06`, which `0791:278A` then blends with form (+0xC) and energy (+0xE) (C-ATTR-03..06). `kicking` and `goal kicking` are one skill on the screen (C-ATTR-04). Hardness, leadership, height, foot and the status words are derived, not stored (C-ATTR-07..10). Fitness (+0xD) and energy (+0xE) are distinct; fitness is read by no match arithmetic (C-ATTR-13). Match strength of a player in the quick engine is `377E` (C-ATTR-14). Performance (+0x12) is a signed running sum (C-ATTR-17).
