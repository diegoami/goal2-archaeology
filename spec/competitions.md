# Competitions and fixtures

Written by the main session at promotion (docs/wide-pass.md, the wide pass of 2026-10-13): rows drafted from reviewed findings F001-F022 by read-only agents, statuses re-derived by the main session (`corroborated` only with two findings and both a behavioural and a static method in the Basis and Findings cells). The summary describes only `supported` and `corroborated` claims.

## Summary

Established for `goal2-a949da2f` (supported or corroborated rows only).
- The game is a loop over the stage byte `[A923]+0x3C` in 2..8; a year ends (ageing and the other year-end
  steps) on entry to stages 2, 3, 5 and 7; the year word increments at 2 and 5 (C-COMP-01, F016).
- Fixture kinds are named by `1771:580B` (friendly, qualifying, play-off, group stage, last 16, quarter-final,
  semi-final, third place, final) (C-COMP-03, F002); the kinds in the set `DS:0F3E` = {3, 4, 7, 8..203}
  (C-COMP-05, F019).
- A shoot-out is played only when the fixture's winner byte is 2 (a draw) and the kind is in the decider set;
  a drawn friendly ends at full time (C-COMP-06, F015).
- Computer-vs-computer fixtures not watched are decided by `000E:6970` (`D47E`, then `B7FA`, `B602`, `D050`,
  `BC5D` for both teams) (C-COMP-10, F010).
- Rankings: each country's points `+0x2F` are group points weighted by `(5 - k)`, placement bonuses 800, 600,
  400, 200 for the world and continents and 200 per knockout fixture; `1771:63A4` sorts and writes the ranks
  `+0x2B` (stage 2) and `+0x2A` (stage 5) (C-COMP-09, F017).
- Tournament top scorers are the per-year goal count `+0x11` (C-COMP-26, F016).
