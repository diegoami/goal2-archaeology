# The match engine

Written by the main session at promotion (docs/wide-pass.md, the wide pass of 2026-10-13): rows drafted from reviewed findings F001-F022 by read-only agents, statuses re-derived by the main session (`corroborated` only with two findings and both a behavioural and a static method in the Basis and Findings cells). The summary describes only `supported` and `corroborated` claims.

## Summary

Only supported and corroborated rows. Pressing `R` runs a different engine from a watched match: a flag `DS:9A3E` makes the tick loop call `0791:D733` (once per game minute) instead of `0791:9564` (C-MATCH-01). Each engine is deterministic from a start state under the key discipline of E004 (keys only at stopped-clock screens); cycles, host speed and the delay calibration do not change a match (C-MATCH-02). Both draw from one 16-bit LCG (C-MATCH-03). The quick engine is modelled seed-exact for the second half of a friendly: per minute it computes eleven-player strengths, line sums, plays 12 attempts per team of three contests each against the opponent's sums, draws goal minutes and scorers, swings player form and decrements energy every fifth minute (C-MATCH-08..11, C-MATCH-13). The watched engine's skeleton, clock, per-tick draw counts and skill reader are established (C-MATCH-18, C-MATCH-20, C-MATCH-23); the contest odds the E011 match reaches are read from the listing (C-MATCH-24..26). Six watched routines (the LCG, its two wrappers, `278A`, `377E`, `287C`) are substituted exactly in whole matches (see the routine table). Performance ratings follow commentary lines through a 67-byte delta table (C-MATCH-29). Energy and form (not fitness) set strength (C-MATCH-11, C-MATCH-15, C-MATCH-16). Rates measured on 100 quick and 100 watched friendlies are in C-MATCH-27.
