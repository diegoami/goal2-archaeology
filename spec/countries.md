# Countries, continents and their strength

Written by the main session at promotion (docs/wide-pass.md, the wide pass of 2026-10-13): rows drafted from reviewed findings F001-F022 by read-only agents, statuses re-derived by the main session (`corroborated` only with two findings and both a behavioural and a static method in the Basis and Findings cells). The summary describes only `supported` and `corroborated` claims.

## Summary

Established (supported/corroborated rows only). There are 128 countries numbered 1..128 (C-CTY-01); `1771:9A40` ranks them in two tables of `GOAL.DAT`: block 8 gives the world rank and block 9 puts 32 countries into each of 4 continents with a rank inside it (C-CTY-02, C-CTY-04), so the continents are Europe, America, Africa, Asia (C-CTY-03). A country's strength is one number, `rating = ((5 - continent)(33 - rank_in_continent) + (129 - world_rank)) idiv 2` (C-CTY-06, corroborated); every squad player of the country starts with ceiling +3 = rating + 0x7F, so strength at a new game depends on ranks only, not on the clock, R007 or R008 (C-CTY-08). The continental means fall in the help file's order, 82.7 / 53.1 / 41.9 / 32.8, with overlapping ranges (C-CTY-07, corroborated). Ranks, hence ratings, are recomputed from competition points (C-CTY-09, C-CTY-10). R008 is a country's default team tactics (C-CTY-13). Two games started in different DOS minutes run differently (C-CTY-20).
