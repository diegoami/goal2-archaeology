The summary describes only `supported` and `corroborated` claims.

## Summary

Established for `goal2-a949da2f` (supported or corroborated rows only).
- A quick result (`R`) of a friendly contains no booking, sending off or injury: 0 in 120 matches, and the
  only foul code (`D5C1`) is not called when the fixture kind is 1 (C-DISC-01, corroborated: F005
  behavioural, F012 static). A watched friendly has on average 1.49 bookings, 0.31 sendings off and 0.41
  injuries (C-DISC-02, F005, n = 100). Goals per match: 1.32 quick against 1.98 watched (C-DISC-03).
- The foul tree is known in outline: `7EEF` books the offender, sends him off if already booked or by a
  1/32 chance, and injures the lower-hardness of the two players with chance about 1/16 (C-DISC-05); in
  a non-friendly quick match `D5C1` is the entry (C-DISC-04). The effects of a booking, a sending off and an
  injury on status bits, confidence, performance and the pitch are set (C-DISC-07 to C-DISC-09, C-DISC-14,
  C-DISC-17, C-DISC-19); a booking does not touch confidence (C-DISC-14).
- A player is unavailable when energy is below 1 or status elements 3, 6 or 8 are set (C-DISC-11); injury is
  the energy store 0 and heals through the post-fixture recovery (C-DISC-13).
- Performance ratings and the man of the match are reproduced exactly from the commentary (C-DISC-17 to
  C-DISC-19, F014).
- The computer's substitutions in a friendly quick match: budget 5, the scan and the rating test (C-DISC-20,
  C-DISC-22, F012, seed-exact on 10 records).
- Team morale on the team panel is `max(1, ((6 + sum of min(32, s8(+0xC)) over the available starters) mod
  65536) div 11)`, recomputed at every panel render (C-DISC-16, F020).
