# Team analysis and rankings

Written by the main session at promotion (docs/wide-pass.md, the wide pass of 2026-10-13): rows drafted from reviewed findings F001-F022 by read-only agents, statuses re-derived by the main session (`corroborated` only with two findings and both a behavioural and a static method in the Basis and Findings cells). The summary describes only `supported` and `corroborated` claims.

## Summary

Summary of `supported` and `corroborated` rows only. A country's rating is ((5 - continent) * (33 - continent rank) + 129 - world rank) idiv 2 (C-TEAM-01, corroborated), from three country-record bytes +0x2C, +0x2A, +0x2B that start from two permutations in GOAL.DAT (C-TEAM-02). The ranks are recomputed from tournament points at stages 2 (world) and 5 (continental) of the cycle, not from skills (C-TEAM-03, C-TEAM-04); they feed the replacement players' potential (+3 = rating + 0x7F) and the confidence expected margin (C-TEAM-05, C-FIT-19). The quick engine's team skills are four line sums of the eleven players' strengths plus a manager bonus, routed by a position-to-line table (C-TEAM-06), which also decides computer-against-computer results (C-TEAM-08). The team panel's skills bars are those sums for side 1 scaled by 11E1 (C-TEAM-07). The team panel's morale is a derived display (C-FIT-27). Player performance ratings are signed table deltas per commentary line (C-TEAM-10).
