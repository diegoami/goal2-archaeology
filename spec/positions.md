# Positions and formation

Written by the main session at promotion (docs/wide-pass.md, the wide pass of 2026-10-13): rows drafted from reviewed findings F001-F022 by read-only agents, statuses re-derived by the main session (`corroborated` only with two findings and both a behavioural and a static method in the Basis and Findings cells). The summary describes only `supported` and `corroborated` claims.

## Summary

Summary of `supported` and `corroborated` rows only. A player has 14 position codes (0..13): goalkeeper, sweeper, centre back, left back, right back, defensive midfield, central midfield, left midfield, right midfield, forward midfield, left winger, right winger, centre forward, striker (C-POS-01, corroborated), kept in +6 (played) and +7 (club position, the one the skills use). Each country's starting eleven and bench positions come from GOAL.DAT block 10 (C-POS-02). Squads are kept sorted by position, with unavailable players last (C-POS-03). A player's twelve skills are derived from +4, +5, +7 through two tables, so positions fix which skills are strong (C-POS-04), and heights differ by position (C-POS-05). In the quick engine position decides which team line a player's strength goes to (C-POS-07) and some tactic overrides (C-POS-08); the substitution picker matches roles through a position table (C-POS-10). The marking row is blank for goalkeepers and sweepers (C-POS-09). No finding gives a formation list, start positions, sweeper drift, or an out-of-position penalty.
