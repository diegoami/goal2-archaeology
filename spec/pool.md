# The player pool and the squad

Written by the main session at promotion (docs/wide-pass.md, the wide pass of 2026-10-13): rows drafted from reviewed findings F001-F022 by read-only agents, statuses re-derived by the main session (`corroborated` only with two findings and both a behavioural and a static method in the Basis and Findings cells). The summary describes only `supported` and `corroborated` claims.

## Summary

A country has a squad of 20 (the first 20 words of its country record; player numbers (c-1)*20+1 .. c*20) (C-POOL-01). The player array has 3061 records, 2560 squad plus 500 unassigned (C-POOL-02). A new game makes the 2560 squad players through `1771:97CC`, reseeding the random generator with the country number, so they are identical in every game (C-POOL-03, corroborated). Only a managed country has a pool: 80 further records numbered 2560 + 80(m-1) + 1 .. 2560 + 80m for manager m, made by `1771:98C4`, sorted by position (C-POOL-04, corroborated); at most 6 managers (C-POOL-05). The SELECT TEAM screen shows the squad view (20) and the pool view (80) (C-POOL-06); the "100 best players" of the help file are 20 + 80 made-up records, the 80 with a lower ceiling (C-POOL-07). Squad slots 1..11 and 12..20 are kept sorted by position and unavailable players sort last (C-POOL-09).
