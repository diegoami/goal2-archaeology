## Summary

The new-game menu accepts 1..6 human managers (C-MULTI-02, F011, supported). Each manager picks a country that is not already taken and gets an own pool of 80 extra players (C-MULTI-03, C-MULTI-04, F011, F007). Aging, retirement and the manager rating handle managed countries separately from computer ones. Turn order, what a second human sees between turns, and fixtures between two human teams are not established.
