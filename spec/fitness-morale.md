# Fitness, energy, confidence and morale

Written by the main session at promotion (docs/wide-pass.md, the wide pass of 2026-10-13): rows drafted from reviewed findings F001-F022 by read-only agents, statuses re-derived by the main session (`corroborated` only with two findings and both a behavioural and a static method in the Basis and Findings cells). The summary describes only `supported` and `corroborated` claims.

## Summary

Summary of `supported` and `corroborated` rows only. A player record holds confidence/form at +0xC, fitness at +0xD and energy at +0xE (C-FIT-01). At a new game fitness and energy are drawn from a table of 16 or 13 plus random terms, capped at 32 (C-FIT-02, corroborated). In a match, energy falls by one point per 5 game-minute step in the quick engine and per more than 180 active updates in the watched engine (C-FIT-03, C-FIT-04); both engines add 2 to energy values 1..30 at the half start (C-FIT-06); a substitute gets no refill (C-FIT-07). At the end of a match energy is set back to fitness for players with positive energy (C-FIT-08), and between matches 1771:3651 moves fitness by two draws and restores energy from it (C-FIT-09). Fitness is read by no match arithmetic; only energy and form are, through 377E (quick) and 278A (watched) (C-FIT-12 to C-FIT-14), and at equal skill a fit and confident side beats a tired and unconfident one 33% to 11% of second halves in the quick engine (C-FIT-15). Form helps up to 0x1F and hurts at 0x20 and above (C-FIT-13, C-FIT-16). Confidence changes by a result term built from the margin and the two countries' rank ratings (C-FIT-19 to C-FIT-22), by fixed event deltas for sending-off, injury and goals (C-FIT-23), and the team morale shown on the team panel is a derived display value that no match code reads (C-FIT-27).
