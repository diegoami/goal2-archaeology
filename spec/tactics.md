# Tactics

Written by the main session at promotion (docs/wide-pass.md, the wide pass of 2026-10-13): rows drafted from reviewed findings F001-F022 by read-only agents, statuses re-derived by the main session (`corroborated` only with two findings and both a behavioural and a static method in the Basis and Findings cells). The summary describes only `supported` and `corroborated` claims.

## Summary

Only supported and corroborated rows. A country's default team tactics are R008, copied by `1771:9A40` into country byte +0x2D: a Pascal set of eight on/off settings, element k at bit k-1 (C-TAC-01, C-TAC-02, C-TAC-04). For elements 1-4 and 8 the bit-to-word map is corroborated by the player-screen tactics rows (80 of 80 screens) and the listing (C-TAC-01); the goalkeeper's swapped words too (C-TAC-03). A player can override elements 1-4 through +0xF (flag at element i+4, value at element i); elements 5-8 have no override (C-TAC-05, C-TAC-06); `1771:3431`/`33CD` derive the default overrides from position and the player's own bytes (C-TAC-07). The quick engine reads +0x2D (elements 1, 5, 6) in its line sums, so a team's tactics move strength from defence to attack by the manager-scaled amount in C-TAC-08; the edit test changed 3 of 4 quick results (C-TAC-08). Elements 2, 3, 4, 7, 8 have no reader in `D733`/`0BC4` apart from the competitive foul roll and marking (C-TAC-09, C-TAC-15). Computer-run teams reset to R008 at set-up and react to the score during a match by fixed rules (C-TAC-10, C-TAC-11). The watched engine reads the effective tactics through `0791:26D7` at ten known sites (C-TAC-13). What each tactic does to a watched match in numbers is NOT established (C-TAC-17).
