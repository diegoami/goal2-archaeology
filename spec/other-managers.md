# The other managers

Written by the main session at promotion (docs/wide-pass.md, the wide pass of 2026-10-13): rows drafted from reviewed findings F001-F022 by read-only agents, statuses re-derived by the main session (`corroborated` only with two findings and both a behavioural and a static method in the Basis and Findings cells). The summary describes only `supported` and `corroborated` claims.

## Summary

For every country there is a country-record marker +0x2E (R015): own number for a computer team, `0x80 + m` for a human manager's (C-MGR-01, F007, supported; `1771:0B77`). A computer team starts every match with its country's default tactics, the byte R008, reset at match set-up by `0791:A3DE` -> `0791:BD83(t)` (C-MGR-02, F010 and F012, supported). During a match the computer changes its tactics only through `0791:C173`, driven by the score difference d, and `C173` skips managed teams (C-MGR-04, C-MGR-05). A computer team's substitutions in a quick result follow a fixed scan (C-MGR-07, F012). Computer squads change by ageing and re-creation of a retiree in place at the ageing gate, not between matches (C-MGR-09, F016, F017). Every country has a manager rating byte that adds to the line sums (C-MGR-11, F012). The help file's "opponents may change tactics" (GOAL.HLP 477-481) is therefore `supported`. Not established: how a computer team picks its eleven when players are unavailable (C-MGR-08), and where a computer manager's name is shown (C-MGR-13).
