## Method (static first)

The named view (`named/seg_0791_07.c` to `_11.c`) and the annotation rows were read first for the callees' names and
the earlier readings (F010, F012, F018, F020, F024, F027), then the whole listing: `code/seg_0791_13.asm` 7CC5-8205,
`seg_0791_14.asm` 82EB-835E, `seg_0791_17.asm` A260-A374 and A920-AC66, `seg_0791_19.asm` BBFF-BC5C, BD03-BEC6, BF6B-C019
and C173-C24C, `seg_0791_20.asm` C5DC-CAAF. The listing decides every line of the translation, written in the
registers it uses (`runtime.Regs`) with T65's to T67's helpers (`guest`, `own`, `player_record`, the stack reads):

- the words pushed for a callee carry the listing's high bytes (`mov al, n; push ax`: 7E60's flag to 9119, 7D56's line
  0x34 and 7CC5 flag word 0xFF01, 8062's notice arguments, BD83's and BF6B's tactic bytes, C173's direction and side,
  C5DC's 1EB5 arguments);
- the words held on the guest stack across a call stay live there: 7CC5's minimum across 2BB1 and 2974 (7D2A, 7D36),
  7EEF's hardness across 2FFB (7F82), A260's doubled index across 2974 (A27A); a callee's first arguments pushed before
  an inner call are pushed here too and handed to the outer call, which writes the same words in the same place: 7D56's
  C88D squad pointer and slot around 2974 (7E1A-7E25), and A9B8's and AAE8's string temporary, which 23E4:075A leaves
  on the stack for 23E4:06E3 (A9E1, AB27);
- the locals the frames write are written (7CC5 writes its own argument word [bp + 0xC] before the clamp 214B:2000
  reads it through SS:DI; C5DC's two flag bytes [bp + di - 6]; the loop counters and pointers of every frame);
- the nested routines read their parent's frame through its BP: C7EB reads C88D's slot, side and squad pointer
  (`ss:[di + 6]`, `+ 8`, `+ 0xA`); BF6B, C0DF and C01A get C173's BP; C88D passes its own to C7EB.

Every call stays a guest call with the listing's target, arguments, return address and near/far form, between these
routines too and to the callees of other rows: T73's notice 7FCF, whistle 0441, delay 0425, the referee 9119, 26C2;
T74's team menu ED8A; T77's 7B77, 6ECF, C48D, C01A and C0DF; the commentary 1EB5, 230C, 2AA3, 2AD4, 2B28 and the notice
C50C; the 214B, 23E4 and 1771 routines. 8062 opens the notice 7FCF and the team menu ED8A for a managed side as guest
calls, so the kit's decision there (the `substitution` context, ED8A's caller return 8103) stops the match with
8062's port frame waiting on its call, T59's mechanism: the sample run of S/injury-70 answered seven such decisions with
the frames live (`out/T68/sample-a-run.txt`), and check.py's interactive and knockout checks resume across them.

The kit's events: 7D56's `inc byte ptr [di + 0x7b87]` is written as 7DD9 (match.py WRITERS["injury"]) and C5DC's `dec
byte ptr [di + 0x9b3a]` as C7DC (WRITERS["substitution"]); `test_port_discipline` checks each writer and that no other
instruction writes those bytes. 8206's fouls count DS:7B84 + 10 side (8274) lies outside the kit's card watch (STATS
+ 7 to + 9). The booking (7A01) and the sending off (7B6D) are T67's and T77's.

No run was needed to write the translation. Runs were made to corroborate it (rule 19) and to count reach (L105).
