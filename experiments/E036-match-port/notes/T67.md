# T67: kick-offs, restarts and set pieces, the walk-off and the booking

Issue #278; route C, P4b. The 25 routines of row T67 (690C to 7B21; the backlog row counts 1,622 instructions) are ported
in `runtime/original_match/port/restarts.py`, beside T59's throw-in 6E11, which is unchanged.

## Method (static first)

The named view (`named/seg_0791_05.c`) and the annotation rows were read first for the callees' names, then the whole
listing: `code/seg_0791_11.asm` from 690C to 6D4B, `seg_0791_12.asm` from 6D4E to 787E and `seg_0791_13.asm` from 7881 to
7B21. The listing decides every line of the translation, written in the registers it uses (`runtime.Regs`) with T65's and
T66's helpers (`guest`, `nested`, `own`, the stack reads), as `contests.py` and `duels.py` are:

- the words pushed for a callee carry the listing's high bytes (`mov al, n; push ax`: the kinds and roles of 6D4E and
  6BF1, the lines of 2AA3 and 6DEA, the CC7B prompt's side and role);
- the words held on the guest stack across a call stay live there: 690C's roll across 278A and 06B1 (6946), 6BF1's
  countdown part across 261A (6D37), 6DEA's statistic across 2996 (6DF8), 7069's first coin across the second (709F)
  and its 2BE5 across 06B1 (70C9), 7621's doubled offset across 2BE5 (7718);
- the locals the frames write are written (6B65's squad pointer and index, 6BF1's record pointer, 6D4E's slot, 717F's
  flag and index, 74C2's saved position, 7621's four bytes, 7881's record pointer and saved flags, 798D's player pointer,
  7A43's count, index, squad pointer and answer);
- the nested routines read their parent's far pointer through its BP (6FEE in 7069, 70F6 in 715A, 71E8 in 7287, 74C2 in
  7621); 717F gets 7287's BP from 71E8 (`push word ptr [bp + 4]`) and reads nothing of it; 690C passes its own BP to
  T66's nested 6755 and 67B6;
- the set test 23E4:08DF answers in ZF, which the `je`/`jne` after it reads (693B, 6C29, 76A5).

Every call stays a guest call with the listing's target, arguments, return address and near/far form, between these
routines too and to the callees of other rows: T66's 6755 and 67B6; T68's 8062 (so a decision under the substitution
check stops the match as anywhere else, T59); T62's 8537 and 8AE9; T73's 0425, 0441, 94E8 and 214B:1E09; T76's taker
prompt CC7B (6D4E calls it for a managed side and the kit answers it as now, F030).

The key poll (7881, the walk-off): `call far 214B:0723` returning to 78F9 and, with a key waiting, `call far 214B:08DD`
returning to 7902 are answered by the kit's logic for that site (`mem.keypressed`, `mem.read_key`, T58; match.py
`_key_waiting`: a walk-off has no key), with the far call's return address written below SP as the call leaves it.
Under a proof (`locate.py`) such a port is refused, as `match.py` `_PortKeys` documents. A pushed-back key at 7902
(DS:79AE set) would run none of 08DD's own frame writes; no match of ALL reaches 78FD (below).

The booking: 798D's `inc byte ptr [di + 0x7b85]` is written through the memory view as 7A01 (`mem.wds8(...,
"0791:7A01")`), the writer the kit reads as the booking event (match.py WRITERS["booking"]); `test_port_restarts` checks
the writer and that no other instruction writes the statistics there. 6DEA's restart count (corners, free kicks) is
written as 6E07.

No run was needed to write the translation. Runs were made to corroborate it (rule 19) and to count reach (L105).

## #336: reset and restart signals

The owner folded goal2's request #336 into this reading (2026-10-19): which write paths and flags identify kick-off,
restarts and set pieces, goal resets and halftime repositioning, and which of them remain observable at the kit's
completed-tick stop. This is static reading of the listing (no run was made for it; the port's family runs corroborate
the translation, not the lifetimes below). Labels: **static** (read from the listing only), **corroborated** (a
behavioural finding or run of this repository also shows it), **unknown**.

**The stop.** The kit samples at the entry of the clock 970C (match.py `_on_tick`, `TICK`). In the watched loop 97D0 the
order is: the replay/snapshot requests (97DF-97F2), DS:9B55 cleared (9801) and DS:7C37 cleared (9806), the sweep 9564
(980B: every pitch record's handler 8AE9 by its state byte, the ball 83A2 and with it the dead-ball dispatch), the
offside line 8CF5 (9815), the referee 9119 (9821), then 970C (9825). So a completed tick shows what the sweep left. Two
loops call 9564 with no 970C after it: 9955 (half_end_sweeps, 9976) and 98C4 (9935-994A); nothing they do between two
of their sweeps is ever seen at a completed tick. 7881's walk-off loop calls 8AE9 and 8537 inside one call, with no 970C
either (static).

**The signal: the pitch record's state byte +0x12.** `Match.pitch()` returns it for each of the 22 records at every
stop. 8AE9 dispatches on it (8B0E-8BB0; T62 `motion.HANDLERS`): 1 kick-off placement 69BD, 2 the kick-off's taker 6AAD,
3 the walk off 6AF7, 5 play 57AC, 6 the after-goal wait 6240, 7 throw-in 7069, 8 goal kick 7287, 9 corner 715A, 0xA
penalty 73BA, 0xB free kick 7621, 0xD chase 58E1. Every write of it in the listing (all instructions writing
`[r + 0x12]` of a pitch record or `[di - 0x7f06]`, the only forms the listing uses; no `rep stos`/`movs` in segment
0791; the set-up's fills of the records, T70's, are not inventoried):

**The flags.** DS:8326 is the clock's 'time is up' flag (970C 973F: set when DS:831B + DS:8321 is 0); with it set the
restarts 6E11, 6E6E, 6F01 do nothing and 69BD keeps every record in state 1 (6A6A). DS:8325 lets the clock run (970C
9716); 83A2 clears it when the ball goes out (8483, T62), 61EC clears it, 65FE sets it, 7881 keeps and restores it. DS:8323
(the taker is ready) is set at the taker's line (705B, 714C, 7259, 7379, 75F6) and cleared at 6AC4, 37C2 (in 34C1) and
F166. DS:8315/8316 is the restart spot (6BF1 6C4D-6D14), DS:830B the holder (6BFA), DS:8302 (set by 6BF1 6C0A and
the launch 34C1 34D5; cleared at the taker's line 7050, 7141, 75EB and at the kick-off 6A92). DS:9B55 (6BF1 6C2B for the kinds in the set at 0791:6BD1, 6ABA at the kick-off) is cleared
at 9801 before the sweep: a value set inside a sweep is visible at its stop and gone after the next sweep's start (static,
as T62's report says). None of these is unique to one class on its own (static).

**Coordinates.** No routine of row T67 writes the points of all records at once: the kick-off and the restarts write
destinations (+7: 69BD, 6BF1's taker, 7069, 71E8, 7621) and the ball (6BF1 6D24 := the spot), and the records walk.
The points written directly: 6AF7 (6B46 := DS:0644, the end of the walk off), 7881 (796C := DS:0640, off the pitch), and
outside the row A2E6 (A2FB := DS:0644 when a pitch record is bound to a player, T62's report). So at the first completed
tick of the second half the records stand where 9955's walk off left them (DS:0644, unless they are DS:830B, DS:830C or
unavailable: 28B2's exceptions), which agrees with goal2's observation of a half's first sample on very few points
(static; that A4A1 binds every record through A2E6 before the first half is T70's and unread here: unknown).

**Conclusion.** A classifier at the completed-tick stop is recoverable statically from what the kit already exposes,
for these classes: the state bytes of `Match.pitch()` give the phase (kick-off placement 1/2/0 until the first all-5;
a restart of kind 7 throw-in, 8 goal kick, 9 corner, 0xA penalty, 0xB free kick, held by every record, a free kick's
wall and a penalty's others at 0; the after-goal wait 6/0), and a restart's kind persists to at least one stop when DS:8323 is clear at 6BF1 (above). Two
distinctions need the kit's events too: a half's kick-off and a goal's kick-off both come from 2929(1) (the half_time /
goal events, or state 6 before it, tell them apart), and kind 8 is both a goal kick (6755 -> 6F01(side, 0), line 0x38)
and a free kick from a foul in the area (6FB6 -> 6F01(side, 1), line 0x3A and the free kicks count +1 at 6E07). The
halftime walk off (3/4) is never seen at a stop, so the second half's first sample is the reset itself. Not established:
any run observation of these values at the stops (no run was made for #336; the family runs record digests, not
`pitch()`), the set-up's own writes before the first half (T70), and the shoot-out. So this is a static result for the
review, not a corroborated classifier; the bounded diagnostic #336 asks for (before/after `pitch()` at the stops of a
kick-off, a half time and one goal and one out-of-play restart) would corroborate it.
