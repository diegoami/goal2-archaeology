# T65: the holder's decisions and the contests

Issue #276; route C, P3b. The seventeen routines of row T65 (4DA9 to 5A8E, 1,404 instructions in the listing's code
lines; the backlog row says 1,400) are ported in `runtime/original_match/port/contests.py`.

## Method (static first)

The named view (`named/seg_0791_04.c`, `_05.c`) and the existing annotation rows were read first for the names of the
callees, then the whole listing, `code/seg_0791_08.asm` from 4DA9, `seg_0791_09.asm` and `seg_0791_10.asm` up to 5AF3.
That listing decides every line of the translation. Each routine is written instruction by instruction in the
registers the listing uses (`runtime.Regs`, as T64's `ball.py` does), so that:

- the words pushed for a callee carry the listing's high bytes (`mov al, 0x11; push ax` keeps the AH the last
  instruction left; 5049's shots, 5565's 2B28 lines, 539F's and 56D9's commentary);
- the words held on the guest stack across a call stay live there (4DFC the holder's roll across 2FFB and 06B1;
  4E50 and 5155 the forward floor across 2BE5; 54A7 and 54D3 the holder's roll and the factor across 278A);
- the locals the frames write are written (the player-record far pointer at 4E44-4E47, every `[bp - 1]`);
- the nested routines read their parent's far pointer through the parent's BP, one level (`ss:[di + 6]`) or two
  (`ss:[di + 4]`, then `ss:[di + 6]`: 539F, 5474, 5513), and 593B and 5A33 write or read their parent's frame
  (59A3 the parent's BP-1, 5A70 the parent's BP-2).

Every call stays a guest call with the listing's target, arguments, return address and near/far form, including
calls between these routines and to the callees of other rows (T66's 5B36, 5E41, 6188; T68's 8206, 82EB; T73's beep
214B:1EA1). Two listing facts the decompiled view hides: 59B3 returns its local byte unwritten when DS:8325 and
DS:8324 are both clear (59C5 jumps over the 59C7 write), and 56D9 always returns 0.

No run was needed to write the translation. Runs were made to corroborate it (rule 19) and to count reach (L105).

## Reach (L105)

`out/T65/coverage-unported.jsonl` is the unhooked kit over ALL with `--count` on 252 addresses: the 17 entries and
every branch target and fall-through of the listing (`coverage-addresses.txt`). The count hooks change nothing: it
compares identical to the T57 reference, 62/62 (`coverage-unported-compare.txt`). `coverage-summary.tsv` gives each
address's total and the number of matches that reach it. Only four blocks are reached by no match:

| block | what | instructions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 4F16 | 4E28: a position 3 or 4 holder with element 1 clear passes (3323) | 5 |
| 5180 | 5049: a strip attempt with the ball moving is a header (3EDF) | 6 |
| 5224 | 5049: near goal, ball moving, 2EF0 true: a header (3EDF) | 6 |
| 53AF | 539F: the holder is a keeper slot: nothing | 1 |

These are the unit tests of `tests/test_port_contests.py`, each with its reached twin. A fifth test checks the live
stack word of 4DFC. `out/T65/test_controls.py` shows the tests fail without the change: with the module absent (as on
`main`) and under five one-line mutations (each tested branch, and the 4DFC push), each run fails on an assertion
(`test-controls.txt`).
