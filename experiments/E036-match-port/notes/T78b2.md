preserving AH; raw seg_0791_05.asm 2CC5–2CE9 establishes the far four-byte
return, ES:DI, CMP flags and one-byte local. Named214B:0688 and raw
seg_214B_01.asm 0688–06AE establish an explicit seed pointer, preserved DS,

assumed the main pointer because only main wrappers called it; the new public
entry must also accept the secondary seed. Its LCG arithmetic is unchanged.

and bounds, main/independent seed pointers, both DF states. Full memory,
all registers/flags and RNG writer PC/value match the unhooked original;
the unrelated seed is explicitly asserted unchanged. All nine helpers execute
in the subprocess that positively denies Unicorn imports.
