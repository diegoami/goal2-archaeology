## CPU a match (parent Done-when 3)

Over the 75, port-v2 (6 matches at a time) against the original engine's run of the same family on
the same machine (`family-original-v1.jsonl`, run alongside): port mean 198.1 s, median 228.0 s,
range 4.5 to 298.2 s, 14,856 s in all; original mean 14.1 s, median 15.3 s, range 0.4 to 23.5 s,
1,056 s in all. The parent's figure for the kit, 9 to 13 s a match, is the original engine. Per
match the port is 13.6 times the original on average (1.7 to 18.6; `play-speed-v1.json`). Quiet and
alone, an F40 match takes 187 s on the port, and E034-03's straight drive 114.8 s against Unicorn's
6.1 s (T117's receipts and #620).

## Play speed (T78d Done-when 4)

`play-speed-v1.json`, by policy and kind: a match played to the end 238 s against 16.7 s; quick
results 5 to 69 s against 1 to 5 s. A fresh goal2 career (E006-r0001, Denmark) has days of 1 to 111
fixtures (`goal2-days-v1.json`, goal2 60548c65); on Unicorn a simulated fixture costs 0.078 s, so
the largest day is about 247 s, nearly all of it the managed match on the port.
`simulate_fixture` (000E:6970) is not ported yet: a day's other fixtures still run on the original
engine and their port cost is unmeasured. The kit runs without a display, so a "watched" match is
the engine's own cost, not a human's pace.
