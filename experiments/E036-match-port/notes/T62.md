# T62: ball and player motion, the offside line and the referee

The eleven routines in row T62 are translated in
`runtime/original_match/port/motion.py`. The pitch picture is engine state: the
player, offside marker and referee writes remain, including restoration of their
old cells from the background picture.

## Method

The sources are the named view `static/goal-exe/named/seg_0791_07.c` and the
instruction listings `static/goal-exe/code/seg_0791_14.asm` (835F through 8CC7)
and `seg_0791_15.asm` (8CCA through 92F9). The listing decides the signed-byte
arithmetic, conditional jumps, call arguments and stack frames where the
decompiler's types obscure them. The family runs corroborate the translation;
they are not the source of its formulas or constants.

Each call outside the row is a guest call at the listing's return address. The
ports preserve the frames and pushes left in the original stack, because SS lies
inside the DS digest. The nested run-step call at 8BB4 receives its parent's BP;
`9119` also preserves the long argument pushed before its random draw.

`out/T62/blocks.py` enumerates the basic-block entries from the listing. Its
unhooked counts distinguish paths reached by the match families from branches
that need synthetic-memory tests. These tests drive the ports with explicit
callee results, checking writes and the guest-call arguments without embedding
the original software.

## Synthetic branches and falsifier design

The tests include the positive goal-line clamp at 867F, state 0x0A's call to
73BA at 8BA1, and the referee's rollback from a blocking character at 92E0.
The state-0x0A expectation names the handler, arguments and return address from
the listing directly: removing the entry must not remove the expectation too.

The three declared variants are `no-draw` (omit the player's new picture cell),
`offside-max` (reverse the line comparison), and `referee-still` (omit the step
and blocking check). Their branch addresses are counted separately before
using their match outputs as falsifier evidence.

### Completed-tick reset evidence request

Issue #336 is a non-blocking request to combine this task's movement reading
with T67's restart paths. This port does not establish a completed-tick
reset/restart classifier. Downstream should keep discrete samples until a
reviewed report establishes signal lifetimes at the actual sampling stop.
That report must distinguish kickoff, halftime and goal/out-of-play resets
from ordinary movement and identity changes. Existing family comparisons
alone do not establish those distinctions.

Static progress for that request is retained in
`out/T62/reset-stop-static-20261019.md` and its adjacent JSON/method/hash
manifest. It verifies the requested kit pin, identifies the actual tick-stop
ordering and inventories 45 direct writes to five candidate flags. Several
candidates are shared with ordinary play, substitutions or consumed pre-sweep
requests. This proposed static report is not a complete classifier or a reviewed
answer to #336; the T67 path/lifetime audit and bounded corroboration remain.
No runtime input or match batch changed.
