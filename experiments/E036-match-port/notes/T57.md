# T57 (P0a): the seed families and the fail-closed comparison on the original-match kit

Issue #268, branch `task/T57-port-families`. Tooling for route C: no game rule is learned here, so no finding. This page
holds no status (the labels do).

## What was built

| file | what |
| --- | --- |
| `records.py` | the record of one match and its grammar (strict JSON, closed keys, whole-string patterns, ranges, every container read to its leaves), the digests (draws per minute, 1 KiB blocks, result without the screen, events), the declared ranges and the mask |
| `families.py` | the families as data, the answer policies, the edits, the discovery of `families/*.json` (and `families/*.py` for a policy a later scenario needs) |
| `families/{F40,O8,S}.json` | the 62 declared matches, each family naming its reference |
| `family.py` | the runner: `--family`, `--out` (resumable, append-only), `--max`, `--workers 1|2`, `--time-limit`, `--count`, `--mask`, `--only`, and `--find-ranges` (the write watch) |
| `compare.py` | the fail-closed comparison (E032's rules for these records), `--expect differ`, `--no-screen`, `--reference`, and `--e032` (the ten E032 seeds against `check.py`) |
| `out/T57/` | the reference, the rerun and every output below, with `mutation_check.py` |

## The families (62 matches)

- **F40** (40): the reference fixture, Denmark (managed) v country 19, calendar number 21, a friendly, from E006-r0001, the
  speed byte written 0 at the captain prompt as E032 did. `F40/e032-*`: E032's ten seeds as they are. `F40/more-*`: ten
  more (0x0042, 0x1111, 0x3A5C, 0x5E5E, 0x7F31, 0x9ABC, 0xB4D2, 0xCAFE, 0xD00D, 0xFACE; chosen as a spread, not searched).
  `F40/edit-*`: the same twenty seeds with the edit set `weak-tired` on country 19 (its +0x2D tactic byte xor 0x2A, i.e.
  elements 2, 4 and 6 flipped; its squad's +4 ability byte -30 (clamped), +0xE energy 12, +0xC form 3; the bytes are
  F008, F021 and E026's). One set for all twenty, as the plan words it ("declared edits ... of one side"). It makes the
  edited side lose and tire: goals 27 in the twenty plain matches against 57 in the twenty edited (E026 saw no tactic
  effect at 30 matches, so the edits are large on purpose).
- **O8** (8): four other country pairs (1-2, 30-44, 64-90, 101-120), the first managed, calendar number 21, each
  played twice: one seed as a friendly (kind 1) and another as a qualifier (kind 2, which adds the competition table's
  update after the match). I read "two seeds each, a friendly and a qualifier" as one of each per pair.
- **S** (14): E033's `stamps.py` scenarios (reference without a seed, `decisions` at pauses 20 and 60 and half time,
  seed 70's injury, seed 4 going in hard for two sendings off, quick results from minutes 30 and 1, a quick result after
  answers at minute 60) and E034's `stamps_competitive.py` (kind 200 at calendar 39 with goal2's budget of 5 and the
  knockout answers, 42 (sudden death), 30 (decided in extra time), quick at half time at 9 and 53, goal2 managing 19 at 42,
  a qualifier). Their answer policies are the classes `Decisions`, `Quick`, `QuickAtHalfTime` and `Knockout` of `families.py`
  (the last calls the kit's `example.knockout_answer`).
- Coverage of the reference (all 62): 7 red cards, 8 injuries, 2 penalties, 44 bookings; extra time in 6 matches, 5 of them
  to a shoot-out; 66 of the game's 67 commentary templates occur at least once; a match is 8,042-267,625 draws.
- A **policy declares the decisions it expects** (`contexts`). A decision outside them is a **stray**: it is answered by
  playing on so the match ends, the record lists it, and the record is not a completed match (this is the kit's
  version of E032's stray key wait).
- A match's **spec digest** covers its fixture, seed, speed byte, budget, edits, policy parameters **and the source of its
  policy's classes**: a reference only compares with a candidate made by the same declaration, and a change to a policy
  invalidates the reference (compare says so: MALFORMED, not a difference).

## The record

One JSON line per match; `records.py` documents every field. Digests and counts only: the commentary's lines, the players'
names and the image's bytes never reach a record (the result's digest hashes the lines, the error text keeps one ASCII line
of the exception without the kit's screen dump). `--count` entries and the mask are recorded; `compare.py` refuses two
masks. The grammar is closed (an unknown key is refused), so a hooked run cannot pass as the unhooked reference: T59 adds its
keys (`ports`, `variant`) to the grammar when it adds the hooks.

## The ranges a match writes (found once, with a write watch)

`family.py --find-ranges out/T57/ranges-20261017.json` played 18 matches that cover the families' paths (F40 plain and
edited, O8 friendly and qualifier, every S scenario) under a Unicorn write hook on all guest memory (61.5 million writes in
a match; 54 s instead of 10 s). Result (program-relative, i.e. the linear address less 16 x the load segment 0x823):

| range | start, bytes | what (basis) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `records` | 262990, 93,648 | the country, player, manager, game and fixture arrays (DS:A917 to DS:A92B + 8 x the fixture count); 79 small spans written inside it. Declared whole, so a write to a record the sample did not touch still shows |
| `w5c8a0` | 379040, 1,776 | many small writes at a fixed stride; DS:7C4C points into it; not named further (read nothing yet) |
| `w5eb30` | 387888, 64 | 64 bytes that three kind-200 matches change (`S/ko-30-extra-time`, `S/ko-9-quick`, `S/ko-53-quick`) and no other match |
| `pitch_background` | 388368, 4,016 | DS:7C1E's block, the background copy the move routine `8E24` restores pitch cells from (E032 notes): 4000 bytes and a header; engine state, so it is compared by `--no-screen` too |

DS (64 KiB, 64 digests) holds the stack as well. The screen is outside both lists: the text page (B8000) and the two
4000-byte text buffers DS:A96E and DS:A972 point at (the second equals the page at the end). The buffers start at DS:ED10, so
DS blocks 59-63 only carry them (`records.SCREEN_DS_BLOCKS`; `--no-screen` leaves them and the board screen's digest out).

**No range is missing**: the write watch saw a sample, so `family.py` also compares, for every match, the whole guest memory
before and after outside DS, the ranges and the three screen buffers, and records an `UndeclaredWrite` error if a byte
changed (a byte written back as it was is no change). All 62 matches of the reference passed it; a match of a later family
that writes elsewhere stops being a completed match and says where (`P+0x...`).

## Judgement calls and things to know

1. **`--count` and the kit's stops.** The first version counted with the kit's `Machine.at` handlers and counted the tick
   routine 3,265 times in a match of 4,146 ticks: a handler that stops the run ends the pass, so a handler registered after it
   at the same address does not run, and the resumed pass is a replay. The count is now a raw Unicorn code hook added after the
   kit's own, which Unicorn leaves to the resumed pass: once per execution (tests: the real match, 4,146 = its ticks). It
   depends on Unicorn 2.1's behaviour; the real-match test is the guard.
2. **Two processes.** `--workers 2` starts its workers from `families.discover()`, not by passing specs, so no policy object
   crosses a process (Python 3.14 starts workers by `forkserver`, which pickles everything it is given). Its test runs the
   script with `--workers 2` on two cheap matches (a few seconds) and compares them with the reference.
3. **A reference older than a policy.** Editing a policy class or a family file changes the spec digest, so the reference
   needs recording again; a harmless edit costs a rerun, the price of never comparing against a stale declaration.
4. **`stopped_at_board`** is the `full_time` event; the kit has no equivalent of E032's `000E` wait strays (it raises), so
   `strays` lists undeclared decisions only.
5. The reference and the rerun were made by the code of commit `5f2d0b2`; `family.py` has changed since (the workers'
   start-up, the `check_writes` switch of the write watch), the single-process path that made them has not, and the final
   commit's compare gives the same output on them.
6. **The tests load the scripts under `e036_*` names** (`isolated_import` in `test_e036_compare.py`): the suite is one
   process and other tests import their own `compare` and `records`. A policy's code digest is the source of its class,
   which `inspect` finds through the class's module, so the modules stay registered under those names.

## Not verified

- Windows, and any Unicorn other than 2.1.4.
- That `w5c8a0` and `w5eb30` are what a later task will call them: only that they are written and their digests are
  compared. The competition tables the task names were not told apart from the other ranges: where a qualifier's table update
  lands is not read (it is inside `records`, DS or `w5c8a0`).
- The ranges for a family that is not in the sample (T74, T76, T77): the undeclared-write check is the guard.
