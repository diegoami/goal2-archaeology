# T64: ball, passing, launching and shooting

The listing decides the translation of the twenty-one T64 routines. The named view is read first, then the raw decompiler output and complete assembly per routine. Whole-match runs corroborate that translation; they do not provide guessed formulas.

## Static sources

The named routines are in static/goal-exe/named/seg_0791_03.c, from2FB1 through4151. The raw decompiled view spans seg_0791_02.c and seg_0791_03.c. The assembly spans code/seg_0791_05.asm throughseg_0791_07.asm. out/T64/static-inventory-20261019.json maps every routine to its named/assembly source and lists the literal call/return sites; the input hashes identify the tracked listings.

## Translation constraints

- RNG call order, including short-circuit paths in3111,34C1,384C and3C3F, is part of match identity. Do not simplify extra draws or replace them with a host RNG.
-34C1 accepts a pitch pointer, event and target. Ghidra obscures set-membership flags and the calling conventions of the long multiply/divide; assembly must decide these paths. Keep low32bits of products and the signed input conversions.
-333F is nested in34C1 and reads the parent frame through a passed BP. Preserve that caller-frame relationship.
-2FB1 pushes its first direction product before a second2BE5 call (3099–30B0). It must be an actual live guest-stack word, because the intervening call observes SP and leaves stack bytes in the final DS digest.
-Each original call remains a registered guest call with literal target, push order and return address, including calls to other routines in this task. Avoid local helpers that bypass the proof event mechanism.
-Memory writes retain listing sites and all picture/board/statistics effects. No screen-occupancy writes are dropped as cosmetic.
