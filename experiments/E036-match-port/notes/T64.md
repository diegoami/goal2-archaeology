## Translation constraints

- RNG call order, including short-circuit paths in3111,34C1,384C and3C3F, is part of match identity. Do not simplify extra draws or replace them with a host RNG.
-34C1 accepts a pitch pointer, event and target. Ghidra obscures set-membership flags and the calling conventions of the long multiply/divide; assembly must decide these paths. Keep low32bits of products and the signed input conversions.
-333F is nested in34C1 and reads the parent frame through a passed BP. Preserve that caller-frame relationship.
-2FB1 pushes its first direction product before a second2BE5 call (3099–30B0). It must be an actual live guest-stack word, because the intervening call observes SP and leaves stack bytes in the final DS digest.
-Each original call remains a registered guest call with literal target, push order and return address, including calls to other routines in this task. Avoid local helpers that bypass the proof event mechanism.
-Memory writes retain listing sites and all picture/board/statistics effects. No screen-occupancy writes are dropped as cosmetic.

### Complete assembly translation and validation method

The port now contains the 21 addresses of the code-only static inventory (1,893 instructions in the original listings). Complete named and raw decompiled views were read first, then every routine's assembly. Where decompiled expressions lose the contract, assembly decides: 34C1 and 3D38 consume guest set-membership ZF, 333F reads a pitch pointer through its caller's SS frame, and 34C1 changes the caller's event word for events 7/8. Guest calls stay guest calls, including long multiply/divide, set tests and calls between ball routines. CS set data is read by the original guest helper, never copied into the port.

Stack words pushed before an intervening call remain live: the first shot coin at 3C54, dribble offsets at 38A0/38F2, and long maximum bounds at 3678/36C8/37CC. Reconstructed full Call argument lists consume only the words belonging to that callee. Signed 16-bit IDIV truncates toward zero after the listing's CWD; long results retain the high word for the following maximum call, then the listing's word/byte stores wrap as written.

The synthetic suite has 33 methods, including both shot directions, the strict pass-adjustment boundary, signed ratio cases, short-circuited draw branches, miscues, and a synthetic long high word passed through division to maximum. Five in-memory controls target the live coin word, helper ZF, offside clock, long high word and live self-target dribble word. Execution is queued against the frozen Python snapshot in unit-input-0a078570ea.json; no passing result is asserted here.

Static translation cannot establish whole-match equivalence or show that a falsifier changes a reached match. The remaining corroboration is a strict first-match smoke comparison, fresh own/integrated ALL families, positive reached-call counts for the three declared falsifiers, and their official comparisons. The variants change horizontal launch direction, replacement acceptance on a zero coin, and shot jitter draws. Their registration is not evidence of reach or detection.
