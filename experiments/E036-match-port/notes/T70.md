## Method (static first)

The annotation rows (F005, F010, F012, F020, F024, F027) and the named view were read first for the callees' names and
the earlier readings; then the whole listing: `code/seg_0791_02.asm` 1073-112B, `seg_0791_04.asm` 2648-269F,
`seg_0791_17.asm` A375-A698, A6E4-A75C, A841-A91F and AC67-AD0E, `seg_0791_18.asm` AD8F-B15E, `seg_0791_21.asm`
D44C-D47D, `seg_1771_07.asm` 517A-52CB and `seg_1771_08.asm` 52CC-53E5 and 580B-59B5. The listing decides every line of
the translation, written in the registers it uses (`runtime.Regs`) as T65 to T68 did:

- the words pushed for a callee carry the listing's high bytes (A3DE's kind byte for 23E4:08DF after the country word
  in AX, A491's side after 1771:34E7's AX, A8CA's colour 5 after 214B:02C8's AX, AD8F's position bytes after `mul`);
- the words held on the guest stack across a call stay live there (AC67's and AFA3's strength across the second
  1771:377E);
- the Pascal string functions' results: A841 pushes 580B's result pointer and 214B:009C's destination itself (580B
  `retf 2` and 009C `retf 4` leave them), and the next call takes them as its first arguments, writing the same words
  at the same place (T61's and T68's way);
- the nested routines read their parent's frame through its BP: AC67, AD8F and AFA3 read B11F's country (`ss:[di +
  6]`) and its bench slot local (`ss:[di - 2]`, which AFA3 writes at AFEA and B0F9); 517A reads 52CC's country and
  swaps 52CC's saved position bytes `[bp + i - 0x15]` with 214B:007B;
- the sets of AD8F (CS:AD0F, AD2F, AD4F, AD6F) and 580B's title strings (CS:577F to 57FF) stay in the guest: the port
  passes their addresses to 23E4:08DF and 23E4:0644, as the listing does, and holds no byte of them.

Every call stays a guest call with the listing's target, arguments, return address and near/far form, between these
routines too and to the callees of other rows: T68's A2E6, A9B8, AAE8 and BD83; T69's 230C; T64's 2FB1; T73's 0712,
214B:0329 and 214B:02C8; T61's 1771 routines; the 214B and 23E4 runtime. The kit's two inputs around the set-up stay
where they were: the seed it writes at the captain prompt comes after 2648's reseed, goal2's budget after A605's.
A4A1's watched writes (the ring counter DS:80EE at A4DA, the minute DS:831F at A621, the budget DS:9B3A at A605, the
statistics through 214B:0018) come before play_match, when the kit's watches take no event.

No run was needed to write the translation. Runs were made to corroborate it (rule 19) and to count reach (L105).
