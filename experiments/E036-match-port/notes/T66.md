# T66: duels, play at the ball, the goal and the after-goal swing

Issue #277; route C, P4a. The fourteen routines of row T66 (5B36 to 67B6; the backlog row counts 1,468 instructions)
are ported in `runtime/original_match/port/duels.py`.

## Method (static first)

The named view (`named/seg_0791_04.c`, `_05.c`) and the existing annotation rows were read first for the names of the
callees, then the whole listing, `code/seg_0791_10.asm` from 5B36 to 6334 and `seg_0791_11.asm` from 6337 to 68E9.
That listing decides every line of the translation, written in the registers it uses (`runtime.Regs`), as T65's
`contests.py` is, and with its helpers (`guest`, `nested`, `own`, the stack reads):

- the words pushed for a callee carry the listing's high bytes (`mov al, n; push ax`: the commentary templates, the
  sign words of 7CC5's calls in 6443 and 65FE, the bounds of 214B:2031 in 67B6);
- the words held on the guest stack across a call stay live there: 5B36's holder roll across 278A and 06B1 (5BCD,
  5C0F) and the ball's long across two calls (5D5B-5D5C, popped into CX:BX for the long compare at 5D72); 5E41's step
  across the coin (5E6F); 6240's other side's score across 2996 (6271); 6337's first slot across 06B1 (6383) and its
  factor or roll across 2BE5 or 06FA (63D8, 6427);
- the locals the frames write are written, at their odd offsets too (6443's record pointer at BP-5 and row pointer at
  BP-9, 65FE's at BP-7, 67B6's at BP-8; 5E41's point bytes at BP-2 and BP-1, written by `add byte ptr [bp + di - 3]`);
  6443 writes its own side argument (64FA, an own goal) and 5F8A its parent's local byte (6188's BP-1, 5FDD);
- the nested routines read their parent's far pointer through the parent's BP: 5F8A one level (6188's), 5F06 two
  (5F8A's, then 6188's);
- the set test 23E4:08DF answers in ZF, which the listing's `jne`/`je` after it read (5CA4, 5D97, 5F3A): the port reads
  the flag the callee left.

Every call stays a guest call with the listing's target, arguments, return address and near/far form (`push cs; call`
is a far call's frame), including the calls between these routines and to the callees of other rows (T65's 4DA9, 5A33,
5A8E; T68's 7CC5, 8062, 8206, 82EB, C173; T73's 0441, 0712; T77's B3B7), so a decision under 8062 (61EC) stops the
match as anywhere else (T59).

The score: 67B6's `inc byte ptr [di - 0x64c4]` at 685C and its `dec` at 68B3 are written through the memory view as
those two instructions (`mem.wds8(..., "0791:685C")`, `"0791:68B3"`), the writers the kit reads as `goal` and
`goal_disallowed` (match.py GOAL_WRITE, CANCEL_WRITE); `test_port_duels` checks both writers and that the goal that
stands is written once.

No run was needed to write the translation. Runs were made to corroborate it (rule 19) and to count reach (L105).
