# T107 revision proposal after the direct probes

Keep the old scoring pilot deferred. T106 corroborates six selected mechanisms, but says
nothing about their natural incidence or net scoring effect. T109 likewise retains the
opportunity/continuation gaps. Replacing the old pilot with another small goal-count comparison
would not answer the most immediate missing question.

This is a design proposal for review, not an executable preregistration, an adopted estimator,
or permission to run games. The owner decision is
[#571](https://github.com/diegoami/goal2-archaeology/issues/571); T107 remains
[#446](https://github.com/diegoami/goal2-archaeology/issues/446), with no completed batch.
The accepted historical T105 records, seeds, estimator and caps remain unchanged.

## First question: does ordinary play enter the interesting mechanism?

Start with a source and existing-evidence inventory. For zero/small launch and far-wall
behavior, follow award/target construction, actual side/clock bindings and the state handler
into the already validated routine. Search retained original traces for the required inputs.
A counter absent from an old record means unobserved, not zero occurrences.

The useful output would be a table distinguishing source-admissible states, genuine retained
witnesses, deliberately synthetic states and missing observations. It could establish which
mechanism deserves further original observation before asking about a score effect. This
inventory needs no engine budget; estimate one small source/trace task before implementation.
It is proposed work, not part of T106's already measured six cases.

## If new observation is still necessary

Design one bounded native observation question, rather than a mixture of reachability,
attribute ranking and scoring interactions. Predeclare complete fixture contexts, target
entry/exit predicates, event ordering, original-key decision policy and what a zero opportunity
means. Verify observer noninterference against a fixed witness. Budget every attempted run,
including failed/refused cells, after an outcome-blind feasibility check of the actual runtime.

For a mechanism question, intermediate state transitions or opportunity counts can be more
direct than final goals. Those outcomes still require a justified sampling unit and precision;
thousands of ticks from one match are not thousands of independent fixtures. A common seed
defines a coupling, not identical random events after trajectories diverge. Synthetic edges
must remain separate from naturally reached observations.

No replacement sample size or spending cap is selected here. The T106 calls used about
6.63 CPU seconds because they bypassed match setup and play; that cost does not justify
extrapolating full-match feasibility or statistical precision.

## If scoring-effect estimation remains the objective

Choose an estimand and a smallest useful effect first. Then demonstrate the proposed design's
precision or detection probability at that effect, using declared assumptions and an
outcome-blind design calculation. The old near-maximal zero-exclusion thresholds and the
unadopted tighter-bound alternative are historical design limitations, not results of matches.

If a defensible interval cannot distinguish useful effects within the available budget,
either leave scoring effects unresolved or propose explicitly descriptive work. Do not relabel
a powerless confirmatory test as evidence of equivalence or no effect. A narrower mechanism
study cannot silently substitute for the old scoring estimand.

## Gate before any spending

The owner selects the revised question and budget after reviewing a concrete, costed design.
That design then needs executable task slices, immutable inputs/sources, completion and
observer controls, stopping/missingness rules and pre-execution independent review. The
current proposal selects no new seeds, estimator, match count or engine backend. ES-R3 can
review the completed T106/T109 results with T107's missing match-effect evidence explicit;
it does not approve this replacement work or complete T107.
