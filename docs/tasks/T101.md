Split into independently mergeable, approximately one-hour slices before dispatch; each gets its own issue, ownership and contract. Validate each slice's Done-when commands as failing on pre-task main and passing on a mock fix. Require source/evidence checks, relevant controls and `python3 tools/check_links.py`; ordinary research outcomes above remain mandatory.
