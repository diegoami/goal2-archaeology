# T119a A route harness: route_fixture under the kit's machine, every write recorded, provable

- **Kind**: infrastructure, the ground truth of U2 (goal2 #509) · **Issue**: #427 · **Branch**:
  `task/T119a-route-harness` · **Merge after**: T118b1
- **Implementer**: the main session (Track B, goal2 #509). **Review**: the milestone's (rule 4).
- **Evidence**: [derived: survey of both clones, 2026-11-03]
  - No harness runs the original outside a match and also hosts the proof. `Match` calls only F1F6 and B7FA, and
    fails on the 000E waits (`match.py:616-617`).
  - goal2's `Core` runs `play_round` and route_fixture under Unicorn and answers every key, but it records no
    writes. Its raw `uc.hook_add` makes `Prover` refuse its machine (`machine.py:46-70`, `prove.py:160-163`).
  - `Prover` needs only a `Machine` (`prove.py:154`), `far_call` (`machine.py:195`) and `run` (397). A port that
    answers keys itself is unprovable (`match.py:151-158`): the harness answers them, as `Match` does.
  - The key sites are catalogued in goal2's `goal2/core/screens.py:820-847`.
- **Owns**: `runtime/original_match/route.py` (new), `tests/test_route_harness.py`; in `runtime/original_match/match.py` only a
  `tail=False` option that ends the match after `F1F6`, before `B7FA` (default unchanged).
- **Design** [derived: `match.py:27-33`, E011-r0001]: `Match` calls `F1F6` with SP 0x3EC0 and BP 0x3EDE, route_fixture's
  BP then. So the harness near-calls `6CA7` with SS = load + 0x3052 and its return word at SP 0x3EE0: route_fixture's
  frame (0x16 bytes) and its two pushes then leave SP 0x3EC4 at `6F18`, and `F1F6`'s frame is `Match`'s exactly.
  At `6F18` the harness snapshots the CPU, plays the managed match with `Match(…, tail=False)` on the machine's
  memory, writes back the memory that match leaves, and resumes at `6F1D` with SP + 4 (`retf 4`). Any write
  `Match` makes before play (its fixture and managed-side set-up) must equal what is there already: the harness
  checks it.
- **Scope**: protects a faithful, recorded run of the original's route_fixture, the ground truth every U2 port is
  proven against.
  - **The harness, `Route(image, keys)`:**
    - It calls `000E:6CA7` as the Design says, on a kit `Machine`, with no foreign hook.
    - It answers each read and poll site of the 000E waits, the gate `6C21` and `6970` from a key policy. The
      default is "a key at the first poll": the owner's decision of 2026-11-03 (goal2 4368fac, #509).
    - It records every write, every RNG step of 7B62 and 8336, and every visit to a key site, in order.
  - The recorded runs from published states are T119b's.
- **Done when**:
  1. `python3 -B -m unittest tests.test_route_harness -v` passes and fails on `main`. From a published state at the
     manager menu with a managed fixture next, one call returns through the managed match: it stops at `6F18`,
     `Match(tail=False)` plays, and it resumes. The machine has no foreign hook, every write is in the record, and
     a key site with no policy answer raises. With the state absent it skips, saying so.
  2. The existing match and port tests pass with `tail` at its default, and CI is green.
- **Hazards**: the crowd animation `045D` draws 1,000–1,500 times per call, and its count depends on the video
  buffer's bytes. Record the draws; never stub the animation.
