- **Kind**: correction/slice; goal2 T170 consumer prerequisite.
- **Issue**: upstream #705 · **Branch**: `task/T130-play-action-entry` · **Merge after**: accepted T127/#699 and T129p/#703, both on main.

- **Evidence**:
  - [confirmed: `runtime/original_match/route.py:87`] `ENTRIES = ("route_fixture", "play_round")`, enforced at
    construction: `if entry not in ENTRIES: raise ValueError(...)`. No entry reaches the manager menu's Play
    action.
  - [confirmed: route.py's own docstring, T124a/#427] `entry="play_round"` calls 000E:70B9 "as main_menu_loop
    000E:7439 does at 77BF", with the BP chain of the published E011-r0001 state.
  - [confirmed: goal2#691's Core oracle, goal2#693] The original's Play action runs `replace_retiree`
    000E:56F3 (called from 57E5) **before** play_round. Compared field by field at the 6→7 transition, the
    original zeroes the retiree (player 2603) and writes a fresh record (2729); the route ages 2603 and
    leaves 2729 empty.
  - [confirmed: goal2#691] Driven from the Core's own memory at 70B9 entry, the route's call is **equal on
    all seven compared fields** — countries, players, fixtures, game block, `[DS:A91F]`, 7B62, 8336 —

    animation under the poll policy. The route is right; the entry is missing.
  - [designed: goal2's owner, 2026-11-09, goal2#693] The consumer invokes the original's own routine rather
    than reproducing the Play action's order, so no reimplementation can drift from it.
- **Owns**: `runtime/original_match/route.py`; `tests/test_play_action_entry.py`;
  `docs/requests/427-route{,-api}.md` where the entry set is documented;
  `experiments/E040-route-fixture-writes/out/T130/`.
- **Scope**: One further supported entry that runs **the Play action's own order** — its pre-step, then
  play_round — on an unstarted route-created Machine. The task protects one claim: **a round played through
  the new entry equals the original's own Play action, and a round played through `play_round` is unchanged.**
  1. **Establish the site from the disassembly and cite it.** 57E5 and 77BF are the sites the existing
     docstrings name around the action; do not assume either is the entry without proving it, and record the
     stack and BP chain as the existing entries do.
  2. **The original's order, not ours.** The entry runs the action's steps in the original's sequence. It does
     not call `replace_retiree` separately, reorder, skip a step, or expose the pre-step on its own.
  3. **The existing entries are unchanged.** `route_fixture` and `play_round` keep their sites, stacks, purity,
     records, news capture, key policy and return contracts exactly. A consumer that does not ask for the new
     entry sees no difference.
  4. **Refusals stay strict.** An unknown entry still raises; the new entry rejects a started, completed,
     foreign or ordinary Machine as the others do.
  5. **No new options.** No seeds, delays, managed/tail/engine or port-body changes, no player policy, no
     table edits, no active saves. This is an entry, not a feature.
- **Done when**:
  1. `tools/heavy.sh t130-controls -- python3 -m unittest tests.test_play_action_entry.Controls -v` fails
     before and passes after: the entry is accepted, unknown entries still refused, lifecycle and target purity,
     and the two existing entries byte-identical in site, stack and return contract.
  2. `tools/heavy.sh t130-proof -- python3 -m unittest tests.test_play_action_entry.ActualEntry -v` passes a
     fresh original-versus-port comparison across the pre-step: the managed retiree is zeroed and the
     replacement written exactly as the original writes them, with ordered draws, keys, pages, memory and both
     RNG words equal, and the complete result, events and resume behaviour unchanged. **A case that covers the
     6→7 transition cannot skip**: it is the one the gap was found at.
  3. The final-source immutable source/hash/receipt verifier under `out/T130` passes; existing route-sealed,
     live-player and substitution controls pass; CI is green. Main and implementer record exact commands and
     tails on the PR; no full local suite.
- **Hazards**:
  - **Changing `play_round`'s contract.** goal2's merged T170a–T170d all depend on it as it is. If the new
    entry cannot be added without disturbing it, stop and report rather than adjusting it.
  - **Reimplementing the order.** If the action's steps are transcribed into the harness instead of run, the
    task's whole purpose is lost, and rule 15's arbiter would eventually catch the drift.
  - **Scope drift into T129p.** The pre-match pool view (#703) merged separately; this task does not touch it.
