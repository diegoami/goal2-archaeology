- **Evidence**: Completed guide, source-pinned inventories, findings, immutable experiments and rewrite recommendation.
- **Owns**: `docs/engine-study/review.md`, `notes/engine-study/T113/` (source census and supporting evidence). The executable slice fixes exact source/annotation ownership before dispatch.
- **Scope**: Independently challenge consequential claims, attribute/control completeness, mathematical examples, experimental validity and defect attribution. Recompute selected summaries and verify that the proposed rewrite contract includes important hidden dependencies. Review a completed prototype only if separately authorised and available.
- **Acceptance outcomes**: A strong independent reviewer supplies a full report with supported findings and explicit unverified items. Findings become concrete issues, corrections are rechecked and the final disposition is recorded on the milestone issue. State exact coverage and limits rather than declaring the engine proved.
- **Dispatch gate / Done when**: this brief records scope and expected research results, not executable readiness. Split into independently mergeable, approximately one-hour slices before dispatch; each gets its own issue, ownership and contract. Validate each slice's Done-when commands as failing on pre-task main and passing on a mock fix. Require source/evidence checks, relevant controls and `python3 tools/check_links.py`; ordinary research outcomes above remain mandatory.
- **Hazards**: A guide being internally consistent is insufficient; compare against original sources and retained evidence. The main session relays findings in full and does not silently weaken acceptance.
