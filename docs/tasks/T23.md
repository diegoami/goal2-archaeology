- **Why**: the bot needs the match engine to choose tactics and squads, and the cheapest faithful
  sampler of results. F005 found that the quick result (`R`) runs its own engine, `0791:D733`, not the
  watched one. F010 traced how the team tactics enter it: `1771:0BC4` moves outfield players' values
  between defence and attack line sums, `0791:B19D` makes three goal contests per attempt against
  those sums, `0791:D5C1` uses element 4, and `D733` draws counters at fixed rates. What is still
  open is the whole rule: what values enter the sums, what each draw compares, how goals and their
  minutes come out, and which RNG stream each draw uses. A model that reproduces the original's
  quick results exactly, seed for seed, settles all of it.
