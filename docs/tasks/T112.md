- **Rating rationale**: Synthesis includes interpretation of game rules and needs hard review. Coordination is medium complexity and the guide is the main user-facing result.
- **Implementer**: main session or the session's selected implementer, using repository tools. **Reviewer**: independent strong-model review for new rule claims; integrated review at ES-R5, then final ES-R5/T113.
- **Review checkpoint**: [ES-R5](../engine-study-reviews.md#es-r5); exact entry, scope and exit criteria apply.
- **Evidence**: All completed study chapters, immutable measurements, issue register and rewrite assessment; T111 only if separately approved.
- **Owns**: `docs/engine-study/README.md`, `notes/engine-study/T112/` (source census and supporting evidence). The executable slice fixes exact source/annotation ownership before dispatch.
- **Scope**: Assemble a coherent reader journey from one tick and one possession to attributes, tactics, lifecycle, empirical effects and rewrite options. Add navigation, glossary, diagrams, worked examples and an exploration atlas connecting questions to recipes and evidence. Record unanswered questions without expanding scope.
- **Acceptance outcomes**: Every accepted field/routine/control inventory item is covered or explicitly unresolved. Separate observed, derived, documented and proposed statements; cite provenance near claims. Reproduce reported summaries from committed inputs and give a reader a concrete route from any attribute to its field effect.
- **Dispatch gate / Done when**: this brief records scope and expected research results, not executable readiness. Split into independently mergeable, approximately one-hour slices before dispatch; each gets its own issue, ownership and contract. Validate each slice's Done-when commands as failing on pre-task main and passing on a mock fix. Require source/evidence checks, relevant controls and `python3 tools/check_links.py`; ordinary research outcomes above remain mandatory.
- **Hazards**: Do not promote proposed findings by repetition, substitute an outline for explanation or depend on the optional prototype to finish the guide. Spec promotion follows the repository's reviewed-evidence process.

Use the [programme's methods, rating definitions and gates](../engine-study-plan.md).
