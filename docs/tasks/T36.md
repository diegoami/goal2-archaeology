- **Kind**: research (static first; the existing quick-engine model computes the effect; at most 6 runs corroborate)

- **Why**: the bot picks its eleven and rests players; it needs to know how much a tired or
  unconfident player costs in a match at equal skill, and the rebuild needs the rule.
- **Evidence** (pasted):
  - Backlog Q018: "Do fitness and confidence change match performance at equal skill? (Q05.10)" —
    hypothesis: at equal skills, a fit, confident side wins more often. Static route: "the readers of
    the fitness and confidence fields in `0791`; `1771:377E` subtracts and adds field +0xC."
  - F012 (quick engine, established): the line sums add `377E(w)` per squad word;
    `377E` = `(+4) + s8(+0xE) − 0x3E`, form `+0xC` ≥ 0x20 shrinks `v − v idiv 16` else adds,
    clamp 0..0x100, wrong foot halves. So the quick result already reads energy (+0xE) and
    confidence (+0xC) through `377E`; `runtime/model/quick_engine.py` models it.
  - F013 (watched engine `9564`, T25): the tick map; which player fields it reads is this task's.
  - F018: +0xD fitness, +0xE energy (energy spent in matches, restored from fitness; 0 = injured).
  - F019/F020: +0xC confidence after a match and events; team morale `DS:9B5A` is display only
    (its one consumer is the team panel).
