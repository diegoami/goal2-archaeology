# Where the defaults of `tools/harness/heavy.sh` come from (#318)

Raw logs are in this folder and are never overwritten; `measure.sh` made the poll and window logs.

| value | where | what was measured or searched |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `heavy.slots` = 4 (harness.json; the script's fallback is also 4) | harness.json, heavy.sh | **Raised from 2 to 4 by the owner, 2026-10-18** (the queue evidence: with two slots busy the load average stayed at 3-5 on this machine, 32 cores and 21 GB RAM, and four implementers' proof runs queued 13 deep and starved a review, #338). **Not measured**: throughput or memory with four matches at once (2, 4 or 8 simultaneous matches were never timed). Measured and relevant: one match's maximum resident set is 128,948 kB (`rss-F40-e032-0101-20261018.log`, `/usr/bin/time -v`, 104% of a core, 9.08 s) and a quick result's 117,236 kB (`rss-S-quick-1-20261018.log`), so the most the four slots can hold, four match processes (a `--weight` job holds that many slots, so workers never add to the four), is about 0.5 GB against 22 GB of RAM (`free -m`, 15.6 GB available when this was written). The original two: not measured as a throughput curve either. It was the limit already written for this machine: `family.py`'s docstring (T57) says `--workers 2` is "the most the shared machine allows". The 2026-10-17 observation in #318: four agents and three reviews at once gave load average 6.8-7, and reviews of the 62-match run took 1-2 hours, where one process alone needs about 14 minutes (the 62 matches in `experiments/E036-match-port/out/T57/reference.jsonl` have `wall_s` summing to about 815 s, mean 13.1 s, max 19.9 s; `cpu_s` mean 14.1 s, so one match is about 1.07 cores). One match, measured: `rss-F40-e032-0101-20261018.log`, 104% of a core, 129 MB resident, 9.1 s (`rss-S-quick-1-20261018.log` is a quick result of 0.33 s, kept, not representative). So two slots use about two cores and 0.26 GB of 32 cores and 22 GB: the limit is a policy that keeps the machine usable for the other projects on it, not a capacity computed from one resource. How throughput changes with 2, 4 or 8 simultaneous matches was **not** measured: the instruction for this task was one match process at a time while the T59 review ran. `slots` is one number in harness.json for the owner to change. |
| poll interval = 1 s (`HARNESS_HEAVY_POLL`) | heavy.sh | `poll-20261018-run2.log`: 6 runs per interval, a waiter queued about 5 s behind a holder that frees the slot at a known time. CPU of the waiter (user+sys, flock, date and stat children included) as a share of one core while waiting: 2 s 0.17%, **1 s 0.30%**, 0.5 s 0.69%, 0.25 s 1.09%, 0.1 s 2.52%. Delay from the slot freeing to the waiter starting, mean (max): 2 s 701 (864) ms, **1 s 593 (919) ms**, 0.5 s 249 (464) ms, 0.25 s 177 (261) ms, 0.1 s 62 (96) ms. A match lasts 13.1 s on average (0.43 s for a quick result, 19.9 s at most; the 62 matches sum to 815 s) and a review or suite lasts minutes, so the mean handover delay of 0.6 s is 4.5% of an average match and nothing next to a review or a suite; 0.5 s would halve it for twice the CPU, and nothing waits on it. The touch interval of `HARNESS_HEAVY_QUEUE_FILE` is the poll. `poll-20261018-interleaved.log` is a first attempt that two copies of the script wrote to at once (my launch error); kept, not used. |
| stale-ticket age = 5 s (`HARNESS_HEAVY_STALE_SEC`) | heavy.sh | A queue ticket is created and locked a moment later; an unlocked ticket older than this is a dead job's and is removed. A live waiter's ticket is locked for its whole wait, so age matters only for that gap. `window-20261018.log`: 2000 tickets, longest create-to-lock gap 2.6 ms idle and 16.4 ms with all 32 cores busy. File times are whole seconds (`stat -c %Y`), so a ticket a few ms old can read as 1 s old, which is why the value must be at least 2; 5 s is 300 times the worst gap measured. |
| `date +%s%N` ticket names | heavy.sh | 19 digits sort as text until the year 2286. |

`tools/harness/heavy.test.mjs` ("the constants relate as their comments say") reads the defaults out of `heavy.sh`, the
slots out of `harness.json`, and the window out of `window-20261018.log`, and fails if the poll stops being far below the
reviewer's idle limit, if the stale age drops under 2 s or under 100 times the measured window, or if the slots in
harness.json and in the script differ.
