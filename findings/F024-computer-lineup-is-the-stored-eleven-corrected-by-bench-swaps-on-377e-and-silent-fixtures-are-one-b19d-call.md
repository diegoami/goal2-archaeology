## Method

- **Static first** (rule 22). The named view lacked `B11F`, `AC67`, `AFA3`, `AD8F`, `B15F`, `C50C`, `D44C`, `A6E4` (`3179` was named by F028 on main in the same period); each was read in `code/seg_0791_17.asm`, `seg_0791_18.asm`, `seg_1771_05.asm` from entry to return, with the nested-procedure frame (`AC67`, `AFA3`, `AD8F` take the caller's `bp` as a static link: `[bp+4]` is `B11F`'s frame, `[di+6]` its country argument, `[di-2]` its local bench slot) worked out by hand, and the data-image tables printed by `tables.py`. - **What the listing does not answer**: that the reading is right (a stack-frame misreading would be silent). That is why the original `B11F` was run against it: `diff_b11f.py` calls the unpacked game's own `0791:B11F` from a far-call frame in Unicorn on the memory of a savestate (nothing hooked, nothing stubbed), and the reading is checked against it on every case; four wrong readings (falsifiers) are caught.
