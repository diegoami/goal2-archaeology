## 2. The unit, the scaling and the records' bound (read, measured)

- **Unit.** A prompt's wait is a number of loop passes, never seconds. `wait_or_key(n)` is `n` polls, each followed by `1E09(10)`, and `1E09(10)` is 10 × `DS:A9FF` passes of its own loop. `DS:A9FF` is the passes of the *calibration* loop in one BIOS tick (54.9 ms) divided by 55, so one "ms" of `1E09` is 1/55 of a tick if the delay loop's pass takes as long as the calibration loop's. The nominal waits are 600 × 10 = 6000 ms (replay, save-goal, summary) and 1000 × 10 = 10 000 ms per pass of the taker prompt. static:static/goal-exe/code/seg_214B_04.asm:57 static:static/goal-exe/code/seg_214B_04.asm:66 E015-r0013

- **Scaling with the emulated CPU speed.** The wait in host time is the nominal wait times (cycles per host second at the calibration) / (cycles per host second now): equal when the game was calibrated at the speed it runs at (a fresh launch at `cycles=fixed 8000`, which is how E015-r0013 was started: r0013 note 1, 05:05:13 UTC; no start state was restored), longer when the speed was lowered after start-up (r0017 note 7, 05:01:05 UTC: about 20 presses of Ctrl+F11), shorter when it was raised or when the game was calibrated at the slow start of `max` and then ran fast. - **Why `N` and `C` are the safe answers (read).** The match loop's key poll `0791:9372` handles Space (the board), Left and Right (match speed), Up and Down (the pitch display), Enter and Esc (the in-match menu), two further function-key codes (0x9F, which draws a screen of unit 16D6 when `DS:9A3E` is clear, and 0xA7, the sound toggle; neither is `Y`, `N` or `C`), and `R` (the quick result); it ignores every other key, `Y`, `N` and `C` included. So an `N` or `C` that arrives after its prompt has expired is read and dropped, and one already waiting in the keyboard buffer when a prompt opens answers it with the default or the computer's pick. `N` and `C` are printable characters in a file-name prompt, so the script never sends them there. A late Enter, Esc, Space, arrow or `R` is not harmless in a match (it opens the menu, the board, changes the speed or ends the match). A script that answers only with `N` and `C` therefore cannot answer by accident and loses nothing if it is late. `Y` is the one key that changes the outcome of a question (it starts a replay or a save and so a file-name prompt) and must be sent only while the question's text is on the page. static:static/goal-exe/named/seg_0791_08.c:32 static:static/goal-exe/code/seg_0791_02.asm:297
- **No fixed sleep and no polling constant.** The waits depend on pace (section 2) and the unlimited prompts never end, so no number of seconds is right at every cycles setting. The script reads the recorded text page, sends its key when the prompt's text is on it (the rows `Do you want ...`, `C-computer select`, `Please enter name of ...`), then reads again and treats the prompt as answered when the text has gone, with no timer of its own for the limited prompts (they leave by themselves). A page read that misses a 6 s prompt is harmless when the intended answer is the default (`N`, `C`); only a `Y` or a typed name needs the text to have been seen, and then the text is checked again before the key is sent.
