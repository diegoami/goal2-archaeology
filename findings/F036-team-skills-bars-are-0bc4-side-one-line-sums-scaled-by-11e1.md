# F036 TEAM skills bars are 0BC4's side-one line sums scaled by 11E1

- **Status**: proposed.
- **Question**: #432 / T114: what the four TEAM "skills" bars (keeper, defence, midfield, attack) show, from which inputs, with which division, clamp and bar scaling.
- **Artifact**: goal2-a949da2f listings; genuine screens of goal2-67cf27d6 (same code and data, F035).
- **Method**: static first: TEAM `1771:157D` and its call of `0BC4` and `11E1`, then `0BC4`, `377E`, `3216`, `3179` (with `3088`, `3041`), `0AA7`, the line table `DS:0D4E`, the Pascal-set helpers `214B:0C57` and `23E4:08DF`, and the highlight writer `214B:13C5` -> `214B:00DB`. The reading answers the question; runs only corroborate it. Corroboration compares the pure decoder with (a) genuine DOSBox-X screens of the owner's sitting E015-r0007, characters and attributes, each paired by its run record's digest with the snapshot holding the memory it was drawn from, and (b) the unmodified original routines run under Unicorn on genuine states. Synthetic controls are labelled as such.
- **Intervention**: none for the genuine screens. Original calls use synthetic call frames and a scratch screen buffer, with no behaviour hooks, each bounded to 2 seconds and 500000 instructions. Synthetic controls edit a private copy of the memory only.

## Answer

- **The panel calls `0BC4(country, side 1, flag 0)` and then `11E1(15)`** at 187E-188F, after the template and every other field is drawn. With flag 0 no eighth is added. - **The line sums are 0BC4's, already C-TEAM-06/F012, now pinned to the panel.** The routine zeroes side 1's four words. Then, for squad slots 1..11 of the panel country with a nonzero word w, it takes `v = 377E(w)`, plus the manager bonus `M = byte[far(DS:A91F)+3*(country+0x2E)+1] div 8 + 16` (0AA7) when v is nonzero. v goes to line `DS:0D4E[+6]`; a line k of 4 or 5 sends `v div 2` (unsigned) to both lines k-3 and k-2. Each outfield player (+6 nonzero) adds `M div 8` per effective tactic element 1, 5 and 6 (the set at CS:0BA4): to attack when the element is set, to defence when it is clear. The effective element e is element e of the player's +0xF when e < 5 and element e+4 of +0xF is set; otherwise it is element e of +0x2D of the country in the player's byte 0. ## Alternatives and limits

1. **The bars are raw line sums, a percentage, or an average of player skills.** The 11E1 division of lines 1..3 by 4 (not the keeper), the 1..256 clamp, the 25/256 scale and the genuine attributes refute it.
2. **goal2's existing line-strength bars can stand in.** They do not apply the panel's side-1 call, flag 0 or 11E1. Use this transform.
3. **The rows show "team quality" independent of selection and tactics.** The XI (slots 1..11 only), availability, form, energy, positions, sides, tactics and the manager's ability all enter, and the in-match screens show tactic toggles moving the bars.

Not established: the original's behaviour for positions whose `DS:0D4E` byte exceeds 5 (positions 16 and up read the next table, and the original then writes outside the four words). The decoder refuses them as a host error. Values the game cannot reach are not claimed to be reachable. Natural states with a sum above 1027 on a divided line, or a keeper above 256, were not observed; only the sweep reaches them. Later sittings' TEAM screens (E015-r0013..r0017) have no published snapshots, so they are not compared.
