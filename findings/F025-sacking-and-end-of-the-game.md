# F025 A human manager is sacked when a lost match leaves the manager's confidence sum at -11 or lower; the shareware limit is a build constant that is off here, so play never ends

- **Question**: Q038 (Q14.1, Q14.2), T51, pass one of the wide pass (`docs/wide-pass.md`): breadth, with an uncertainty per rule.
- **Experiment and runs**: E025 (`experiments/E025-sacking-and-end/`): static only, no game run. `searches.py` and its committed output `searches.log` are the searches behind every "never" below.
- **Artifact**: `goal2-a949da2f`, the registered build (F001, `artifacts/known.json`); `GOAL.EXE` unpacked as in `static/goal-exe/README.md`.
- **Tests claims**: C-END-01..08 (`spec/end-of-game.md`); the table at the end maps each one.
- **Confidence**: unreviewed (status `proposed`). Every rule is read from the listing (`static/goal-exe/code/`, cited `file:line`) and has not been run against the original. ## Answer

### 1. The sacking decision (rule 1)

- **The measure is a signed byte per manager and per country, `[A91F] + 3*k + 2` (call it the confidence sum), not the rating byte R016** (`+1`). For a managed country `k = 0x80 + m` (the country's `+0x2E`, `1771:0B77`); for a computer country `k` is its own number. `0791:B6CC` adds a signed amount and clamps to -127..127 (`seg_0791_18.asm:979-1007`, compare at `:996`); `1771:63A4` folds it into the rating byte `+1` (clamped 1..128) and zeroes it (`seg_1771_09.asm:948`, `:956`), for every country, in the same loop as the rank recompute. Established/read.
- **What feeds it** (`000E:6CA7`, `seg_000E_09.asm:296-352`): after each fixture that a managed team plays in (`F1F6`), unless the fixture kind (byte `+2`) is 1 = friendly (`:6F34`), `B71C(fixture, side)` is added to the entry of the `+0x2E` of both sides (`:6F80`, `:6F86`). `B71C` is F019's: `(country_rating(opponent) - country_rating(own)) idiv 32`, plus the absolute score margin if the side won and minus it if it lost, none for a draw, doubled when "(side won) = (side is 0 or `5A03` says the kind is in `DS:0F3E`)". So a loss is negative: the rank term (about -3..+3, ratings are 1..128) minus the margin, doubled in some cases. The computer sides accumulate it too.
- **When the sum is zeroed**: at each entry to stage 2 or 5 except the first of a new game (`1771:80DA` raises the year word `[A923]+0x38` before `1771:6730` runs; `6730` calls `63A4` only when the word is above 0: `seg_1771_09.asm:1111-1113`). So the sum is "since the last stage-2/5 gate".
- **Where it is judged** (`000E:4E0E`, `seg_000E_06.asm:549-725`): `6CA7` calls it once for each human side after the match and its news (`seg_000E_09.asm:383`). It builds the news pages (`11D9`, `4DB7`, `26F2`) and, if a flag it initialised to 0 at `:557` was set by one of them, calls `unmanage_country` `000E:01FA` for that manager (`:708`, `:719`). The only store that ever sets the flag in the whole listing is `seg_000E_04.asm:825` (`searches.log`, first block: the other `ss:[di-2]` stores in segment `000E` belong to the fixture gate `6C21`, called from `6CA7`'s own frame). The only call of `000E:01FA` is `:719` (`searches.log`, second block).
- **The sack branch** (`000E:319B`, called by `4DB7`, `seg_000E_04.asm:133-`): reached for a human side only on a **loss that is not a shoot-out**: the fixture's winner byte `+3` is neither the side nor 2 (draw) (`:153-160`, `:646-648`), and the two score bytes differ (`:735-745`; equal scores with a winner mean a penalty shoot-out and give the "drama" headline). Then `000E:0B77` returns the signed score `s` (below) and (`:755-825`):

  | s | effect |
  | --- | --- |
  | -7, -6 (`0xFFF9`, `0xFFFA`, `:756-762`) | headline "THINGS LOOK GRIM" (`CS:3086`) |
  | -10..-8 (`:785-790`) | "YOUR JOB IS / ON THE LINE!" (`CS:3097`, `CS:30A3`) |
  | -255..-11, in practice -127..-11 (`:805-806`) | "<manager> / IS SACKED!!!!" (`CS:30B0`), then the flag at `:825` |
  | -5 or higher, or `s` = 0 | an ordinary defeat headline (`CS:30BE ..`), nothing else |

  The threshold is therefore **the sum at or below -11 after a lost, non-shoot-out match**; the match that crosses it is included (the delta is added before `4E0E` runs).
- **The gates in `0B77`** (`seg_000E_02.asm:146-196`, a different routine from `1771:0B77`): it returns 0, so no warning and no sack, when (a) the year word `[A923]+0x38` is below 2 (`:160`); (b) the fixture kind is 1 (`:164`); (c) the stage byte `+0x3C` is in `{3, 4, 7, 8}` (`1771:59D8`, set bytes `98 01` at `seg_1771_08.asm:735`) **and** the per-manager byte `[A923]+m+1` is non-zero (`:175`); (d) the country's byte `+0xBE` is non-zero (`:179`). Otherwise it returns the sum, sign-extended (`:194`). Gate (a) is a grace period: the year word is 0 at a new game and rises at each entry to stage 2 or 5, and the displayed year is `+0x3A + 2 * word` (see 3), so no one is sacked in the first four displayed years.
  - (c): `60CB` shows "FAIL TO QUALIFY" when the same byte is 0 and "QUALIFIES FOR / GROUPS ARE ANNOUNCED" otherwise (`named/seg_000E_05.c:54-100`), so `[A923]+m+1` reads as "the manager's country has qualified": a team in the finals stages is exempt. The writer of the byte was not found (see Limits). `likely`.
  - (d): nothing in the listing writes or reads `+0xBE` apart from this compare (`searches.log`, the `0xBE` block); it is the last byte of the 0xBF-byte country record and reads as always 0. `likely`.
- **The strings the drafts listed are headlines, not rules.** `000E:5E6C` (called by `60CB`) writes the news item "<country> FAIL TO QUALIFY FOR ..." with a random one of "BOO HISS", "SACK", "GET RID OF" and the manager's name (`seg_000E_07.asm:926-1010`, data at `5E1F`): it changes no state. `000E:2071` (called twice by `26F2`) and `26F2` are further news writers called by `4E0E`; none stores into `4E0E`'s frame. `DS:59C9` " SACKED " is in the screen template at `DS:591E` (see below).
- **What being sacked does** (`000E:01FA unmanage_country`, `named/seg_000E_01.c:82-143`): the flag `[A923]+0x47+m` is set; the manager's own record gets `+1 = 1`; the country is released to the computer (`+0x2E` := its own number, so `1771:0B77` no longer says managed); its squad positions and the manager's 80 pool players are reset; its fixtures with first byte `0xFE` become `0xFF`. After the headline `4E0E` waits for a key (`seg_000E_06.asm:707`). The manager stays in the menu loop: the manager screen draws the template `DS:591E` ("Tough luck. You have been SACKED for incompetence", strings `DS:5965..5A04`) instead of the team (`seg_000E_09.asm:718-` `721E` with `DS:591E` and the flag), the squad and tactics items do nothing for a flagged manager (`named/seg_000E_05.c:939-978`), the stage-announcement loop skips him (`named/seg_000E_05.c:723`), and item 6 still plays rounds (`:987`). **No new country is offered**: the only mode of the country picker that assigns a country (`1771:26B9` mode 2) is called only from `1771:2D15` at a new game (`searches.log`, last block). If every human is sacked the game keeps running as spectator play. `read`; the screen's picture was not rendered.
- **The offered welcome** "APPOINT A NEW MANAGER ... WELCOME ABOARD" (`000E:68A4`) is shown once per manager at a new game (`seg_0000_01.asm:47`), not after a sacking. `read`.

### 2. The shareware limit (rule 2)

- **It is three build constants that are off in this build.** Each is `mov al, N / or al, al / je|jne` with a literal `N` (Turbo Pascal folds `if Shareware ...`): `000E:7072` (`seg_000E_09.asm:453`, `N` = 0) returns "the limit is reached" as `flag and (year word >= 2)` (`:457`); `1771:8208` (`seg_1771_12.asm:136-142`, `N` = 0) refuses to advance the stage byte when the flag is set and the stage is 8; `1771:090F` (`seg_1771_01.asm:1015-1017`, `N` = 1) asks "Do you want to save this game" only when its flag is set. Fifteen such constant tests exist (`searches.log`); the three here are the ones tied to the shareware text. The rest were not read. `read` for the constants; that the shareware build holds the opposite values is `inferred` from the help text (GOAL.HLP 154-156) and the strings.
- **What the limit does when set** (`7072`'s three users): `70B9` (`seg_000E_09.asm:493`) skips the stage dispatch and the announcements after `8208`; the manager screen (`:718`) draws the template `DS:19D6` instead of the team: " GOAL ", " NE'92 ", " On shareware. ", " register this ", " game to play ", " for unlimited ", " seasons with ", " load and save ", " game facility " (`strings.tsv` lines 746-754); main-menu item 6 (`:1175`) only cycles to the next manager and plays nothing. With `8208`'s own constant set, the stage byte would stay at 8 and never wrap.
- **The limit in the units of the game**: the first four displayed years are one pass of stages 2..8 (the displayed year is `+0x3A + 2 * word`, `seg_000E_07.asm:555-566` and `seg_1771_08.asm:942-957`; the word is raised at stages 2 and 5). In this registered build neither constant is set, so nothing stops, and the save prompt of F10 appears (consistent with F001's two saved files). The 8208 constant and the 7072 test (`word >= 2`) both stop at about the same point (end of stage 8); which of the two a shareware build reaches first, or whether both, was not worked out. `read`.

### 3. Does play end? (rule 3)

- **Terminal conditions searched, none found in this build.** (a) Every access of the year word `+0x38`, the displayed year `+0x3A` and the stage byte `+0x3C` is listed in `searches.log`: the compares are against 0 (new-game and first-year tests), against 2 (the two gates above, `0B77` and `7072`), and against the stage numbers 2..8 as set tests (14 compares); `+0x3A` is read only to print a year (`:59C5`, `5B7D`, `1C9E/1CD6`) and written once, at a new game (`seg_1771_14.asm:397-403`). No compare of a year against a limit exists. (b) The stage byte wraps `8 -> 2` through `214B:1D6F(1, 8, 2)` unless the 8208 constant is set (`seg_1771_12.asm:154`). (c) Every caller of the Halt routine `23E4:00D8` (`calls.tsv`, eight callers in `searches.log`): the end of the main program (after the menu returns), the manager-count menu `1771:0021`, F10 quit `1771:090F`, the "no country left" path of `1771:2D15`, and run-time-library error paths (`214B:08DD/0A10/1869/206A`). None tests the game state. (d) Sacking releases one human; it does not stop the game (see 1). So in this build play ends only when the player quits. `read`; "never" here is limited to the access patterns searched (direct `es:[di + 0x38]/0x3A/0x3C` reads and the Halt callers); an indirect read through a precomputed pointer other than `[A923]` is not excluded.
- **The F016 puzzle is closed**: the year word rises at stages 2 and 5 (twice a cycle) but the displayed year is `base + 2 * word`, so each rise is two displayed years, i.e. four years per cycle. Why the ageing also runs at stages 3 and 7 (F016) is not answered here.

## Inferences

- A manager is "sacked if performance is very poor" (GOAL.HLP 154-156) = confidence sum <= -11 (since the last gate) after a defeat in a non-friendly match, with a grace period and a finals exemption. Typical size: a 0-1 loss to an equally ranked team adds -1 (-2 for side 1 in a kind outside `DS:0F3E`, by the doubling predicate), so about 6..11 such losses between two gates; a win adds the same amount back. `inferred` from `B71C`'s formula (F019), not run.
- The two warnings (-7..-6, -10..-8) have no effect other than the headlines. `read`.

## Limits and what was not done

- No run. The cheap probe the task allowed (an unhooked savestate read of `[A91F]+3*k+2` after a lost match to see the accumulation and the sack) was not taken: it needs a managed savestate near a threshold, which does not exist in `.cache/kept` and costs a session of play to make; the whole chain is therefore `read`, not `measured`.
- The writer of `[A923]+m+1` (the qualified flag, gate c) was not found. Searched: every `mov byte ptr es:[di], 1` / `[di + 1], 1` within 14 lines of a load of `[0xa923]` (only `[A923]+1 := 1` at `seg_000E_09.asm:483` and `:1188`, which is the current-manager byte `60CB` also writes), and every `mov byte ptr es:[di], al|cl` within 16 lines of one in the 1771, 000E and 0791 segments (none); stores by `214B:0018` (fill) or `214B:003C` (move) with computed pointers are not excluded. Its meaning rests on `60CB`'s use of it. `likely`.
- Whether the shareware build's constants are all 1/0 as inferred is not testable here (only the registered file exists).
- `11D9`, `26F2`, `2071`, `4DB7`'s other callees were read for stores into `4E0E`'s frame (none), not for their headline logic.
- The layout of the screens `DS:591E` and `DS:19D6` was read from `strings.tsv` and the call sites, not rendered.
