## Inferences

- **"Sacked if performance is very poor" (GOAL.HLP 154-156) is the sum <= -11 since the last gate, after a defeat in a non-friendly, non-shoot-out match, with a grace period and a finals exemption.** The number of losses this takes depends on the ratings: a strong side losing narrowly to a weaker one gains `-margin` plus a negative rank term (several matches), a weak side losing to a much stronger one can gain. Not run. `inferred`.
