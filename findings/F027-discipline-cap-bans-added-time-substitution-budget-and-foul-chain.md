## Answer

- Every card, every injury and every red-card-or-injury removal comes from `0791:7EEF` (`798D`, `7B77` and `7D56` have no other caller, calls.tsv:1055-1057), and `7EEF` has two callers: the watched foul `8206` (new reading: it is the foul sequence, not a substitution display) and the quick engine's `D5C1`. static:static/goal-exe/code/seg_0791_13.asm:739 static:static/goal-exe/code/seg_0791_13.asm:1082 static:static/goal-exe/code/seg_0791_21.asm:623
- **Cap**: `7A43` allows a red card or an injury while `DS:7B86 + DS:7B87` of the side is below 3 (managed side) and, for a computer side, while more than 11 of its 20 squad words are available: at most 3 players per side per match. Polarity checked on both callers. A booking is never asked.
- **Bans**: `B27F`, run after every fixture on every squad word of both teams: element 8 cleared; a sent-off player (element 6) gets element 8; a booked player (element 5) gets the carried-booking element 4, or, if 4 was already set, element 8 and 4 cleared. Element k is bit k - 1 (`214B:0C57/0C79/0CA1`). So a red card is a one-fixture ban, the second booking since the last ban is a one-fixture ban, and a booking in the same match as a red card is forgotten (`7B24` clears element 5).
- **Added time**: `DS:8321` counts bookings + sendings off + injuries (both sides, both halves), at most 5, and is paid only at the end of the second half; the first half ends when `DS:831B` reaches 45 whatever `DS:8321` says. 20 of 20 quick halves under the stand-in end at `DS:831F = 91 + min(5, events)`.
- **Budget**: `A4A1` sets `DS:9A3D = 0` for a fixture whose byte +2 is 1 and 1 otherwise, and `DS:9B3A[side] := DS:066B[DS:9A3D]` = 5 (kind 1) or 2 (every other kind), both sides alike; `C5DC` decrements it. The computer's substitution rule `8062` is the same in both engines; the quick engine asks it once a minute without a goal, the watched engine at every dead ball and half start (E011: 26 asks, 2 substitutions).
- **Foul table**: `DS:8337` holds rand(16) - 1; `7EEF` reads two bytes per foul, draw1 < 4 (1/4) books or, if already booked, sends off; otherwise a 1/32 chance sends off; draw2 == 1 (1/16) injures the lower-hardness player (a tie the victim). F005's 1.49 / 0.31 / 0.41 per watched friendly are what about 6 fouls per match give (inferred); E011's recorded match has 3.
- **Injury duration**: 1 in 3 per fixture for a squad processed by route `3651`, at once for a squad processed by `3558`; which route a fixture takes is read, not run.
- **Year change**: nothing writes the discipline elements at the year change; a ban and a carried booking persist until `B27F` clears them (a retired and re-created player starts clean).
- **Confidence**: the help file's "bookings damage confidence" is not in the binary: `798D` writes no `+0xC`.
