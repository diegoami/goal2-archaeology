### 1. Formations: there is no table, no name and no id (read)

### 2. The formation label on the team panel (read)

- `1771:0E6C(country, dest)` counts, over squad slots 1..11, the players that `1771:3216` says are available, by the class `DS:0D5C[+6]` = 0 for the goalkeeper, 1 for codes 1..4 (sweeper, centre back, both backs), 2 for codes 5..11 (the three midfield and the forward midfield codes **and both wingers**), 3 for codes 12 and 13; it writes `defence-midfield-attack` as three numbers joined by the two-byte string constant at `1771:0E6A` (`01 2D`: length 1, `-`) into a string that `157D` draws at column `0x21`, row `0xC` of the panel. The sweeper therefore counts as a defender and the wingers as midfielders; unavailable players are not counted, so the label of a squad with an injured man shows fewer than eleven. **read** (not emulated; the label's screen position is the named view's `draw_team_panel`, no screen was read). static:static/goal-exe/code/seg_1771_02.asm:411 static:static/goal-exe/code/seg_1771_02.asm:458 static:static/goal-exe/code/seg_1771_02.asm:473 static:static/goal-exe/code/seg_1771_02.asm:495 static:static/goal-exe/named/seg_1771_02.c:68 static:static/goal-exe/data/ds_2581_01.hex:113
