- The seed of a new game is the DOS hour and minute: 10 launches at DOS time 10:00:00 reached one main screen and one seed word (E003-r0007 … r0016), and 2 launches at each of 10:01, 10:02, 13:37, 21:00 and 23:58 reached a screen per minute, five different screens (E003-r0017 … r0026); the seconds, which differ between the launches (`DS:7B64`), do not change the screen. Free launches carry the host's minute (E003-r0032, r0033).
- The DOS year is a second input: at 10:00:00 with the DOS date 10/04/1993 the same seed word gave another main screen (year 1994 instead of 2028, another team and rankings), with DOS's read-back confirming the date in E003-r0047, r0048 (E003-r0043, r0044, r0047, r0048 against E003-r0007).
- D3's difference is the moment the keys reach the live match, not the clock: after a restore the DOS clock is the savestate's (10:00:12.8 … 12.9 in all 18 restores, DOS time fixed or free; E003-r0001 … r0006, r0027 … r0029, r0034 … r0042) and the seed words are equal through the keys before the match (E003-r0001 … r0006, r0034 … r0039); from the key that starts the match, the number of draws before `r` differs between runs of one batch, and so does the quick result (7 distinct screens and 8 distinct seed words after `r` in 12 runs, E003-r0001 … r0006, r0034 … r0039). With the starting Enter and `r` sent together, 6 of 6 runs in two batches 10 minutes apart gave one result, one end screen and one seed word (E003-r0027 … r0029, r0040 … r0042).
- How a later task fixes or records each input: the DOS time and date — set them (driver config `dos_time`, `dos_date` MM/DD/YYYY, I3) and record the run's `DS:7B62`; or restore a savestate made after the new game started, which brings its own clock and seed (E003-r0001 … r0006). The live-match timing — record `DS:7B62` before and after a match; to fix it, give the quick-result key with the key that starts the match (arm d3b, E003-r0027 … r0029, r0040 … r0042), or, for a played match, treat key timing as input and record it. The second stream and the delay calibration — recorded by any savestate (guest memory); the stream feeds the speaker only (static).

## Method

- Machine and tools: as F003 (Ubuntu 24.04 in WSL2, DOSBox-X 2024.03.01, Xvfb, xdotool, Python 3.12.3; each record's `runtime.fingerprint`), `cycles=auto` (F001's determinism probes ran at `auto`), `svga_s3`, memsize 16. Driver `runtime/driver/driver.py`; the runner `experiments/E003-q001-inputs/run.py`; `code_commit` in each record; the code under `runtime/driver/` and `experiments/E003-q001-inputs/` changed at `848147e` (r0001 …), `34f8fac` (`summarise.py` only, r0004 …), `9df6e3a` (arm d3b, r0027 …) and `df0dee7` (date form `MM/DD/YYYY`, `DS:A9FD` read, r0034 …); the commits between them add records only.
- DOS time (I3): with `--dos-time`, the autoexec runs `time HH:MM:SS` (and `date ...`) before `goal`. The clock was read back in every record: the BIOS tick count (`0040:006C`) at the first read, converted at 1193182/65536 ticks per second (`summarise.py`), was 0.7 … 1.1 s after the time set in every fixed launch. The date: r0001 … r0033 and the `main` state passed `10-04-YYYY`, which DOSBox-X's `DATE` ignores (Anomalies); from r0034 `MM/DD/YYYY`, seen in the year word `DS:A9FD`. From r0045 (review rework round) the driver refuses an impossible date or time, and the autoexec writes DOS's own `date /t` and `time /t` to `CLOCK.TXT` on a drive D: (the session folder) right after setting them; `Driver.launch` compares it with the request (date equal; time −1 … +5 s in r0045 … r0048, −1 … +2 s from rework round 3: the upper bound from the clock at the title, 0.7 … 1.1 s past the time set in every fixed launch, E003-r0007 … r0026, r0030, r0031, r0043, r0044, r0047, r0048, plus one designed second) and aborts the run with `ClockNotApplied` otherwise; the I3 text says what DOS reported, or that the clock was not verified (E003 README, amendment 4).
- GOAL.EXE's data segment, found in each savestate (`runtime/driver/savestate.py`, `find_program`): the memory control block whose owner is the next paragraph and whose name field starts `GOAL`, followed by a PSP (`CD 20`); load segment = PSP + 10h; DS = load segment + 2581h (`static/goal-exe/README.md`, "Data segment 2581"). In every record: MCB 0812, PSP 0813, load segment 0823, DS 2DA4, linear base 0x2DA40; the bytes at `DS:0024` (`07 54 52 59 20 4f 55 54`, "TRY OUT") and `DS:00C0` (`6f 6e 20 66 6f 72`, "on for") equal the listing's data segment (`static/goal-exe/data/ds_2581_01.hex`).
- Arms (keys copied from F001's `runtime/spike/probe.py`): `d0`: fresh launch, then `n Enter Enter b o b Enter Enter Enter Enter Esc`, each key followed by a settle poll (screen changed, two reads agree); `d3`: launch, restore `main` (Host+L, polled to its digest), then `Esc Up Enter Enter Enter c` [2.0 s delay] `r Enter Enter r`; `d3b` (added after r0026): `Esc Up Enter Enter Enter+r Enter Enter r`, `Enter+r` being two key presses with no poll between. Every read records `DS:7B62`, `DS:7B64`, `DS:8336` (and `DS:A9FD` from r0034).
- Start state `main`: a fresh launch at DOS time 10:00:00 (host date 2026-10-04), the keys `n Enter Enter b o b Enter Enter Enter Enter`, Host+S (`run.py make-main`); sha256 `20c5bd8b…`, screen `18190236…`, `DS:7B62` `0xee42`, `DS:7B64` `0x0935`, `DS:8336` `0x0057`; kept in `.cache/states/driver/`.
- The Answer table is per lead of T06 (behavioural citation; static citation in `static/goal-exe/code/`).
- Static: the listing `static/goal-exe/` (capstone, unpacked sha256 `6fd8c5ef…`); `experiments/E003-q001-inputs/static_refs.py` over the unpacked image (relocation table and `B4 2C` scan).

### Anomalies

- DOSBox-X's `DATE` ignored `10-04-1993` and `10-04-2026` (r0001 … r0033 and the `main` state): it printed `Current date: Sun 10/04/2026 / Type 'date MM/DD/YYYY' to change.` (a check outside the records, the same command lines), and r0030, r0031 gave the main screen of r0007. Those records' I3 text names a date that was not set; their DOS date was the host's, 2026-10-04 (launch times in the records). Records are not edited (L207); `runtime/driver/driver.py` now refuses a date not in `MM/DD/YYYY`.
- A date of the right form but impossible, `99/99/1993`, was also accepted and declared set by the code of r0034 … r0044 (review R1; a local run of the reviewer, numbered 51, not in this repository): DOS kept the host's date. Repeated as a development check with the refusal bypassed (not a record): DOS read back `10/04/2026` and `9:59:59`, `DS:A9FD` `0x07ea`. The driver now refuses it before any run, and checks DOS's read-back in every clock-setting launch. Of the date records before the read-back existed, r0043 and r0044 show the year applied at `DS:A9FD` (`0x07c9`); r0034 … r0042 requested 10/04/2026, the host's date that day, which no record verifies but none could tell apart.
- DOS reads a time set to 10:00:00 back as `9:59:59` (DOS time is BIOS ticks; the 10:00:00 ticks `0x000a004a` are 35999.98 s): E003-r0045 and r0046 aborted with `ClockNotApplied` under the first version of the check (0 … +5 s), their I3 text "NOT verified as applied", their read-back `10/04/1993 9:59:59`. With 1 s early allowed, E003-r0047 and r0048 completed with that read-back.
- Drive D: (the read-back's) was first mounted only in clock-setting launches; a `main` state made with the clock option then crashed DOSBox-X (code -11 during a savestate) when restored with the clock free (review round 2 R1, the reviewer's local runs, not in this repository). From rework round 3 every launch mounts D: and reads the clock back (the read-back is in every record from E003-r0049); with a `main` state made by `run.py make-main --dos-time 10:00:00 --dos-date 10/04/2026` (sha256 `813cf85f…`, screen `18190236…`, as before), `run.py d3b --runs 2` with the clock free completed twice (E003-r0049, r0050) with d3b's result `2e282d3e…`, end screen `641ecfc6…` and `DS:7B62` `0xa797` after `r`, as E003-r0027 … r0029, r0040 … r0042. The earlier `main` state (sha256 `20c5bd8b…`, made without D:) is kept aside in `.cache/`; a state made without D: restored into a session with D: stalled DOSBox-X's restore (review round 3, the reviewer's local runs: "savestate not complete within 15s"). ## Inferences

- The DOS time seeds the game when a new game starts: on the behavioural route, the main screen and `DS:7B62` are a function of the DOS hour and minute in 26 launches, with `DS:7B62` exactly 162 LCG steps past hour·256+minute in every one (E003-r0007 … r0026, r0030 … r0033, r0043, r0044); on the static route, `23E4:11D7` stores INT 21h AH=2Ch's CX at `DS:7B62`, the LCG's seed word (Answer, leads 1–4). Both routes: corroborated once reviewed. Only the low word is used, so the seconds are not an input (E003-r0007 … r0016: `DS:7B64` differs, the screen does not).
- The DOS year is an input of a new game, separate from the seed (E003-r0043, r0044, r0047, r0048 against E003-r0007 … r0016: one seed word, two screens; the date confirmed by DOS in r0047, r0048); the static route gives one path, GetDate's year → `DS:A9FD` → `[A923]+0x3A`. What else the year changes (the team offered by the same keys) is not established here.
- A restored savestate fixes the clock and the seeds: they are guest memory (the BIOS tick count, `DS:7B62`, `DS:8336`), so the DOS time set at launch is overwritten by the restore (E003-r0001 … r0006, r0034 … r0039: clock 10:00:12.8 … 12.9 fixed or free). F001's D4 (restored title, same screens across batches) is consistent with this.
- The input behind D3's difference is the moment keys reach a live match, measured in the game's own running: the live match keeps drawing from `DS:7B62` while the driver waits, and `0791:9372` acts on a key only at its next poll, so the draws before `r` depend on when `r` and the preceding keys arrive (E003-r0001 … r0006, r0034 … r0039; static `seg_0791_15.asm:726`–`874`). Five runs reach one seed after `r` (`0x8c07`, 6876 draws: E003-r0003, r0005, r0006, r0035, r0036) from before-`r` reads at 3082 … 3146 draws, while E003-r0039, read at 3115, reached 5696: the read before `r` is a snapshot of a match that runs on until `r` is acted on, and several arrival moments lead to the same poll. Removing the wait removes the difference (E003-r0027 … r0029, r0040 … r0042). F001's within-batch identity and between-batch difference are consistent with a settle poll that stopped at the same moment within a batch and at another under the other batch's machine load (F001 names another project's DOSBox-X running at 08:31); not tested.
- The second stream (`DS:8336`) is set by the boot tick count, so it follows the DOS time at launch and is held by a savestate (E003-r0007 … r0026, r0001 … r0006); the listing sends its draws only to the speaker (`0791:94E8`), so it is not a candidate for D3's result: it is equal in all twelve d3 runs before and after `r` (E003-r0001 … r0006, r0034 … r0039).

## Alternatives considered

- "The DOS time read through GetTime (`235C:00A2`) or the CMOS clock is a further input": excluded on the static route (no reference, direct or through a stored pointer; no `int 0x1a`, no port 70h/71h); not tested behaviourally (E003 never changed the time without changing the seed).
- "D3 differs between batches because of the DOS clock": excluded by E003-r0001 … r0006 and r0034 … r0039 (restored clock equal, fixed or free; results differ within one batch as much as across batches).
- "D3 differs because of the restored seeds": excluded: `DS:7B62`, `DS:7B64`, `DS:8336` equal at the restore and through key 4 in all twelve (E003-r0001 … r0006, r0034 … r0039).
- "The savestate reads themselves change the match" (I2 not neutral): not excluded; a read during a live match stalls emulation for its duration, which is itself part of the key timing; d3b's identity (6 of 6) shows reads before the match do not change the result.
- "The cycles setting or host load changes the live match's pace": consistent and not separated from key timing; `214B:1DA7` calibrates a busy-wait from emulated speed, not tested.
- "The seconds of the DOS time are an input": excluded for the new-game seed (E003-r0007 … r0016); not tested for other uses of `DS:7B64` (the listing references it only at `23E4:11D7`, `datarefs.tsv`).

## What this does not establish

- The `000E:70B9` reseed (the start of a later qualifying stage) was not reached by any run, and no saved game was loaded: when `1771:080C` returns non-zero the entry point skips `1771:76B1` (`seg_0000_01.asm:18`–`21`); what `1771:080C` tests, and whether a `.GL` file carries the seed, are not established.
- The year's effect beyond the main screen of E003-r0043, r0044, and any use of the month or day (GetDate's month and day are not stored, `seg_214B_03.asm:1067`–`1085`).
- Whether a played (not quick) match depends on key timing in the same way; whether `cycles` other than `auto` change the live match's draws per second; whether d3b's identity holds on another machine or under other load (2 batches, 10 minutes apart, one machine).
- The meaning of the 162 draws between the reseed and the main screen, of the 32187 draws of the results screen, and of any draw (Q002–Q036).
- Other DOSBox-X versions (the `DATE` form and the savestate layout are 2024.03.01's).
