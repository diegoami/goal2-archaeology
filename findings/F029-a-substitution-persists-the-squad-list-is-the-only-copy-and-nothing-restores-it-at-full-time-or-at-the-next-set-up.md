- **One line: it persists.** Nothing in the match set-up, the match or the post-match code copies the squad words and writes them back; a substitution of the managed side is two squad words swapped in the live country record, and the swapped eleven is the eleven of the next match (measured for one manager, Italy, in two careers: 3 substitutions in 2 reloads of one saved game, section 2; the same-iteration replay exception of section 1 is unverified).
