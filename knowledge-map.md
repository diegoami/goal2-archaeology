# Knowledge map

What is known about each area of *goal (version 2)* (artifact set `goal2-a949da2f`), and how it is
known. An area is `unknown` until evidence moves it: to `documented` when G4 records a help-file
passage as a source (basis `doc`: asserted by the help file, not yet tested), and beyond that only
through a reviewed finding and its promotion into `spec/`. Statuses and basis tags follow
game-archaeologist's `vocabulary/` at the commit [`archaeology.json`](archaeology.json) pins.

The Source column links each area to the passages of [`sources/goal-hlp.md`](sources/goal-hlp.md)
that describe it, labelled with their `GOAL.HLP` lines (in the released file, CR LF stripped). The
Status column links to the area's candidate research questions, which are predictions the help file
implies and are not judged. An area that stays `unknown` says why in its Status cell.
